---
title: Annual Vacation
description: Criteria for requesting, approving, rescheduling and managing instructors' annual vacation across all units and regions.
---

This procedure explains how annual vacation works for Palms Sports instructors within the UAE Military Sports Program. It covers four stages: **request, approval, rescheduling and management**.

The rules apply to **all units and all regions** of the project and exist for three reasons:

- Keep all military bases operating.
- Guarantee minimum instruction coverage at all times.
- Give everyone the same chances during high-demand periods.

<Callout type="warn">

The bases must keep operating and the contract must be fulfilled. This takes **absolute priority** over any individual interest.

</Callout>

---

## Structure: Units and Regions

The project is organized into **units** and **regions**:

| Term | Definition |
| ----- | --------- |
| Unit | Division of the project by force (e.g. Land Force, Naval Force, Military Police or another defined by the project). |
| Region | Group of bases in the same geographic area, under the responsibility of a Team Leader. |
| Base | Military installation where the instructor teaches the classes. |

The project controls vacation limits **at both levels**. The unit limit protects the contract as a whole. The regional coverage rule protects the day-to-day classes at the bases.

---

## Request Deadlines

Vacation follows a **permanent annual cycle**:

| Milestone | Deadline |
| ----- | ----- |
| Submission of requests for the following year | **October 30** of the current year |
| Review and decision | By **November 30** of the current year |
| Vacation validity | January – December of the following year |

<Callout type="error">

Anyone who submits a request **after October 30 loses their position in the priority queue**. These requests are only reviewed after all requests submitted on time have been processed.

</Callout>

## Responsibilities

Each role has its own duties within the vacation process:

<Tabs items={['Instructor', 'Team Leader', 'Supervisor', 'Project Manager']}>

<Tab value="Instructor">

- Request vacation **within the deadline**.
- Provide correct information.
- Buy tickets **only after official approval**.
- Complete the full handover before leaving.

</Tab>

<Tab value="Team Leader">

The Team Leader operates at the **region** level.

- Assess the operational impact per base and per region.
- Check for coverage conflicts.

</Tab>

<Tab value="Supervisor">

The Supervisor operates at the **unit** level.

- Make the final approval or rejection decision.

- Guarantee contractual compliance with the military client.

</Tab>

## Approval Criteria

Approval is **not automatic**. Each request goes through the criteria below, in this order of priority:

1. Chronological order of request submission.
2. Operational availability of the unit **and** the region during the requested period.
3. Maximum limit of simultaneous absences per unit and/or region.
4. Critical operational need of the base or the region, such as events and evaluations.
5. The priority rules in this procedure.

---

## Operational Limits

### Unit Limit — The 20% Rule

No unit may have more than **20% of its instructors on vacation at the same time**. If **20%** of the current headcount does not result in a whole number, **round down**.

### Regional Coverage

The unit limit is not enough. The Team Leader must also guarantee that **no region falls below minimum coverage**. Each base needs one of these two things:

- Enough instructors for all scheduled classes.
- A documented substitution plan.

**The most restrictive criterion always prevails.**

### Limits Table (Template)

Each unit maintains its own version of the table below and updates it whenever the headcount changes:

| Field | Content |
| ----- | -------- |
| Unit | Name of the unit. |
| Region(s) | Regions covered by the unit. |
| Current Headcount | Number of instructors in the unit. |
| Max. Simultaneous | **20%** of the current headcount, rounded down. |

<Callout type="warn">

The Supervisor or the Project Manager may make these limits more restrictive at any time due to contractual necessity, headcount changes or operational risk.

</Callout>

## Priority Rules

### High-Restriction Periods

The periods below are in high demand across the entire project. During them, coverage requires special attention:

| Period | Reason |
| ------- | ------ |
| **July and August** | Coincide with school holidays in the Emirates. |
| **December** | Festive season with simultaneous high demand. |

### Priority for School Holidays

During school holidays (July/August and December), priority goes to the instructor who:

- Has school-age children.
- Has a spouse employed under a contract that follows the school calendar.
- Met the request deadlines.

Within this priority, **whoever submits first has preference**.

### Option A or Option B

The most contested periods need to be divided fairly. For this reason, each instructor chooses **one option per annual cycle**:

| Option | Guaranteed Priority | No Priority |
| ----- | -------------------- | -------------- |
| **Option A** | July / August | December |
| **Option B** | December | July / August |

<Callout type="warn">

The instructor must declare their choice **at the time of the request**. It applies to the entire current annual cycle and cannot be changed afterwards.

Instructors without priority can still take vacation in the other period, if there is operational availability.

</Callout>

### Vacation Start Aligned with the School Calendar

An instructor who requests July or August because of the family's school calendar must start the vacation on the **first possible day after the school release** of the spouse or dependents involved.

The instructor must confirm this start date and record it in the request.

---

## Splitting the Vacation

The instructor may split the annual vacation into **up to two periods** and may reserve **5 days** to participate in competitions.

Splitting depends on three conditions:

- Prior approval by the Supervisor.
- Operational availability in both periods.
- Minimum base coverage maintained in each period.

---

## Buying Tickets

An instructor entitled to a company ticket must request it **at least 60 days** before the departure date.

<Callout type="error">

The instructor may only buy tickets **after the formal approval** of the vacation.

</Callout>

---

## Mandatory Handover

Every instructor must complete the handover **before the vacation starts**. The handover is **mandatory** for release.

The handover must cover these five items:

| Item | Expected Content |
| ---- | ----------------- |
| Base status | Current state of the classes, schedules and student attendance. |
| Administrative information | Pending documents, late deliveries and open communications. |
| Pending reports | Missing weekly reports and unsubmitted KPIs. |
| Operational needs | Required substitutions, special coverage and upcoming events. |
| Student and command pending items | Students with special needs and unanswered command orders. |

<Callout type="error">

Anyone who does not complete the handover may have their vacation departure **delayed or blocked**.

</Callout>

---

## Document Alerts

The Team Leader must frequently check the validity of all instructors' documents, especially in the months before the vacation period.

| Document | Early Alert | Required Action |
| --------- | ----------------- | --------------- |
| Emirates ID | 90 days before the vacation | Renew before departure — it can block operations and re-entry. |
| Gatepass | 90 days before the vacation | Renew — without a valid gatepass the instructor cannot access the bases. |
| Passport | 6 months before expiry | Start the renewal process with the consulate. |
| Residence visa | 3 months before expiry | Coordinate with the company's HR/PRO department. |

<Callout type="error">

The Supervisor **does not approve** vacation when the instructor has a document that is expired, or that expires within **90 days** of the requested period. The only exception is when the instructor confirms that the renewal is already in progress.

</Callout>

## Approval Flow

<Steps>
  <Step>

  ### Request Submission

  The instructor submits the request with the dates and the preferred period (Option A or B). If requesting priority, the justification must be included.

  </Step>

  <Step>

  ### Team Leader Verification

  The Team Leader verifies:

  - The unit limit and the regional coverage.
  - The documents and the coverage conflicts.
  - The handover and the priority history.

  </Step>

  <Step>

  ### Recommendation

  The Team Leader issues the recommendation: approval, adjustment or rejection.

  </Step>

  <Step>

  ### Supervisor Decision

  The Supervisor reviews the recommendation and decides. If rejecting, the Supervisor informs the instructor and explains the reason.

  </Step>

  <Step>

  ### Notification and Tickets

  The instructor receives the final decision. If approved, the instructor may then buy the tickets.

  </Step>

  <Step>

  ### Handover and Departure

  The instructor completes the handover and documents everything. The Team Leader confirms the coverage and the instructor leaves on vacation.

  </Step>
</Steps>


## Rescheduling

The instructor may request the rescheduling of an already approved vacation. Rescheduling depends on four conditions:

- Formal justification sent to the Team Leader.
- Operational availability in the newly requested period.
- An open slot within the unit and region limits.
- Supervisor approval.

<Callout type="warn">

**There is no guarantee of approval for rescheduling.** The instructor assumes the risk of not getting another date.

</Callout>

---

## Cancellations Due to Operational Necessity

In exceptional situations, the company may request the change or cancellation of an already approved vacation. These situations are:

- Operational need.
- Contractual requirement.
- Project need.

In these cases:

- The company seeks a new date in agreement with the instructor.
- The rescheduling follows operational availability.

<Callout type="error">

Once the vacation has been applied and approved in the system and by HR, the instructor may only cancel or reschedule it by presenting a **plausible justification**. Each case is reviewed individually.

Outside these conditions, cancellation is **not accepted** and the instructor keeps the approved dates.

</Callout>

---

## What to Take from This Procedure

If your situation is not covered here, do not decide on your own. Take the case to the Supervisor, who forwards it to the Project Manager when necessary.

The operational limits in this procedure are not fixed. They may be updated when the headcount changes or when the contract requires it, and this update may change the availability of slots in the periods you intend to request.

Changes to already approved vacation follow what is described in [Cancellations Due to Operational Necessity](#cancellations-due-to-operational-necessity).
