---
title: About this Manual
description: Official operational guide for instructors of the Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program.
---

This manual is the official operational reference for all instructors working in the Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program. It establishes the procedures, standards, and responsibilities that must be followed across all training bases to ensure consistency, professionalism, and operational excellence.

The manual provides guidance on daily instructor responsibilities, including attendance procedures, dress code, professional conduct, scorecard approval, promotion testing requirements, booklet management, and other administrative and operational processes.

Every instructor is expected to understand and apply the procedures described in this manual. Following these standards helps ensure:

- Consistent instruction across all training facilities.
- Fair and standardized student evaluations.
- Accurate administrative records.
- Professional conduct and appearance.
- Compliance with Palms Sports operational standards.

<Callout type="info">

This manual is intended to be used as a day-to-day reference. Instructors should consult it whenever they have questions about procedures, operational requirements, or their responsibilities within the Jiu-Jitsu Program.

</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">

The procedures described in this manual are considered the official standard for the Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program. All instructors are expected to follow them at all times while performing their duties.

</Callout>
