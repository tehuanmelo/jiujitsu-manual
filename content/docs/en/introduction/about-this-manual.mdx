## Program overview and mission

The Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program aims to deliver high-quality instruction with standardized methods, fair evaluations, professional conduct and consistent procedures at every training base.

Each instructor contributes directly to the development, safety and experience of the students, and also represents Palms Sports while performing their duties.

This manual is the official reference for the work of the instructors. It sets out, in a clear and objective way, the main guidelines, responsibilities and procedures of the Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program. Its guidance must be known and applied at every base, to ensure safety, quality of instruction and operational consistency.

What this manual covers:

- Instructor conduct and dress code;
- Communication hierarchy and chain of command;
- Daily attendance and student attendance rules;
- Stamps and booklet management;
- Scorecard approval and minimum test requirements;
- Injury Report and Incident Report;
- Reports and documentation;
- Organizational roles and operational procedures;
- Competitions and student participation.

## Core principles

All the guidance in this manual is based on five core principles:

| Principle | Application |
| --- | --- |
| **Safety** | Protect the physical well-being of the students and ensure that activities take place in appropriate and safe conditions. |
| **Professionalism** | Represent Palms Sports and the Jiu-Jitsu Program with integrity, respect and a professional attitude. |
| **Consistency** | Apply the same standards, criteria and procedures at every base and in every class group. |
| **Accountability** | Keep accurate records, follow the established procedures and complete the required documentation within the deadlines. |
| **Leadership** | Lead by example and promote discipline, respect, teamwork and continuous improvement. |

## How to use this manual

When carrying out any task or procedure:

1. Check the corresponding section of this manual;
2. Follow all the established steps and requirements;
3. Use the examples, templates and reference images when available;
4. If in doubt, ask the Head Coach or your immediate superior in the chain of command.

<Callout type="warn">

**Important:** If any guidance in this manual conflicts with an instruction the instructor has received, the instructor must follow the guidance of their immediate supervisor and report the discrepancy through the established chain of command.

</Callout>

## Purpose of the procedures

The procedures set out in this manual aim to ensure:

- **Student safety** — all students train in appropriate and safe conditions;
- **Fair evaluations** — the same criteria and standards are applied to all students;
- **Accurate documentation** — relevant information about classes and operations is properly recorded;
- **Professional instruction** — classes are organized and conducted according to the quality standards of the program;
- **Operational consistency** — procedures and work standards are maintained across all class groups and bases;
- **Shared responsibility** — all team members work together toward the same goals.

Meeting these standards helps preserve the quality of the program and the trust of the students and of the institutions served.

---

## Instructor responsibilities

Each instructor is responsible for:

- Teaching classes safely, professionally and in accordance with the established Program;
- Keeping accurate administrative records and completing reports and documents within the set deadlines;
- Following the dress code and the standards of professional appearance;
- Respecting the chain of command and using the proper hierarchy to handle operational matters;
- Checking that students meet all the established test requirements before approving or signing scorecards;
- Promoting discipline, respect, teamwork and a culture of continuous improvement;
- Promptly reporting any situation that could affect student safety, the quality of classes or the operation of the program;
- Maintaining a professional attitude during all activities related to the Jiu-Jitsu Program.

---

## Instructor daily routine

The daily routine of the instructor must include, as applicable:

- Arriving at the base in proper uniform, prepared and on time;
- Checking that the training space and conditions are appropriate and safe;
- Conducting classes according to the standards and content defined by the program;
- Recording student attendance correctly;
- Stamping booklets, when applicable;
- Completing the required reports and documentation;
- Reporting injuries and incidents immediately, when they occur;
- Checking test requirements before approving or signing scorecards;
- Reporting any relevant occurrence or situation to the appropriate superior in the chain of command;
- Following the chain of command for all operational matters.
