---
title: About this manual
description: Official operational guide for instructors of the Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program.
---

This manual is the official operational reference for all instructors working in the Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program. It establishes the procedures, standards and responsibilities that must be followed at every training base to ensure consistency, professionalism and operational excellence.

The manual provides guidance on instructors' daily responsibilities, including attendance procedures, dress code, professional conduct, scorecard approval, promotion test requirements, booklet management and other administrative and operational processes.

Every instructor must understand and apply the procedures described in this manual. Following these standards helps ensure:

- Consistent instruction across all training facilities.
- Fair and standardized student evaluations.
- Accurate administrative records.
- Professional conduct and appearance.
- Compliance with Palms Sports operational standards.

<Callout type="info">

This manual is meant to be used as a day-to-day reference. Instructors should consult it whenever they have questions about procedures, operational requirements or their responsibilities within the Jiu-Jitsu Program.

</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">

The procedures described in this manual are considered the official standard of the Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program. All instructors must follow them at all times while performing their duties.

</Callout>
