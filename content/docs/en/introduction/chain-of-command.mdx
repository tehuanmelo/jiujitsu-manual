---
title: Chain of Command
description: Communication hierarchy and reporting procedures for all instructors.
---

The Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program follows a defined chain of command to ensure effective communication, efficient decision-making and clear accountability at every training base.

Every instructor must respect this hierarchy and communicate through the appropriate level of management.

<Callout type="warn">

Always contact your **immediate supervisor** first. Do not skip the chain of command unless specifically instructed or authorized to do so.

</Callout>

## Purpose

The chain of command defines who reports to whom. Following this structure ensures operational matters reach the right people quickly and are resolved at the appropriate level.

Each level of management holds specific responsibilities, authority and information relevant to its role.

---

## Communication Hierarchy

| Level | Role | Primary Responsibility |
| -----: | -------- | ---------------------- |
| I | **Head Coach** | Direct supervision of the operation at the base and on the mat. Monitors class execution and instructor performance day to day. |
| II | **Advisor** | Multi-base role: monitors Head Coaches and instructors across different units and acts as the direct point of contact between the operation and the company. |
| III | **Team Leader** | Consolidates and triages the information received from the bases, forwarding to supervision the matters that require a management decision. |
| IV | **Supervisor** | Direct interface between the company and the field operation. Turns corporate directives into action in the area and is accountable for the results achieved. |
| V | **Technical Manager** | Full management of the technical operation. Oversees every level of the structure, ensuring alignment, standardization and operational continuity. |

---

## Communication Guidelines

When communicating about operational matters, instructors must:

- Contact their **immediate supervisor** first.
- Follow the established chain of command at all times.
- Escalate an issue only when instructed to do so or when the immediate supervisor is unavailable.
- Use only the company's official communication channels.
- Keep the immediate supervisor informed about matters affecting students, classes, operations, safety or administration.

---

## When to Follow the Chain of Command

The chain of command must be followed whenever an instructor needs:

- Technical guidance.
- Operational support.
- Schedule changes.
- Approval for leave or absences.
- Administrative assistance.
- Reporting of incidents or injuries.
- Student-related guidance.
- Approval for operational decisions.
- Any matter requiring management involvement.

---

## Why the Chain of Command Matters

Following the chain of command provides several important benefits:

| Benefit | Description |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Efficient Communication | Information reaches the appropriate person without unnecessary delays. |
| Faster Resolution | Issues are handled by the person responsible for that level of authority. |
| Clear Accountability | Each level understands its responsibilities and decision-making authority. |
| Better Organization | Communication follows a consistent, professional process at every base. |
| Operational Consistency | Procedures are applied uniformly across the entire Jiu-Jitsu Program. |

---

## Typical Communication Flow

In most situations, communication should follow this sequence:

1. **Instructor**
2. **Head Coach**
3. **Advisor**
4. **Team Leader**
5. **Supervisor**
6. **Technical Manager**

Instructors normally communicate with their **Head Coach**. If an issue requires a higher level of management, it will be escalated through the chain of command.

---

## Prohibited Actions

The following actions are **not permitted** unless specifically authorized:

- Contacting upper management without first informing your immediate supervisor.
- Skipping one or more levels of the chain of command.
- Requesting approvals from a higher authority without following the proper reporting process.
- Ignoring instructions from your immediate supervisor.

---

## Exceptional Situations

If your immediate supervisor is unavailable or has specifically instructed you to contact another member of management, you may communicate directly with the appropriate person.

Whenever possible, keep your immediate supervisor informed about any communication made outside the normal reporting structure.

---

## Summary

Following the chain of command promotes professionalism, improves communication, speeds up problem resolution and ensures every matter is handled by the appropriate level of management.

Respecting this process is every instructor's responsibility and contributes to the success of the Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program.
