## Role descriptions

### VI — CEO / General Manager

The CEO / General Manager is responsible for the company's strategic direction. The person in this role sets the organizational objectives and ensures the company operates efficiently to achieve the best possible results.

---

### V — Technical Manager

The Technical Manager oversees the organization's technical operations and technical team, and is responsible for hiring, performance evaluation, promotions, career development and disciplinary actions.

---

### IV — Supervisor

The Supervisor monitors daily operations and instructor performance, and ensures that company procedures are followed.

---

### III — Team Leader

The Team Leader coordinates the instructors in their assigned group or area, including schedules and the communication of organizational information. The Team Leader also reports incidents and accidents involving students, as well as instructor performance.

---

### II — Advisor

The Advisor provides technical and operational support to instructors and Team Leaders, and advises management on administrative and technical matters.

---

### I — Head Coach

The Head Coach is the instructor's direct supervisor and the first point of contact in the chain of command. The Head Coach provides technical leadership to the instructors at the assigned base and escalates issues to upper management when necessary.

<Callout type="info">

For operational matters, instructors must always contact their **Head Coach** first and follow the established [chain of command](./chain-of-command).

</Callout>
