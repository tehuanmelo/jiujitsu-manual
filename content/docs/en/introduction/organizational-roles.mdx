## Role Descriptions

### VI — CEO / General Manager

The CEO / General Manager is responsible for the company's strategic direction. This role sets the organizational objectives and ensures the company operates efficiently to achieve the best possible results.

---

### V — Technical Manager

The Technical Manager oversees the organization's technical operations and is responsible for:

- Hiring new employees.
- Evaluating employee performance.
- Managing promotions and career development.
- Applying disciplinary actions when necessary.
- Overall management of the technical team.

---

### IV — Supervisor

The Supervisor is responsible for monitoring daily operations and ensuring company procedures are followed.

Responsibilities include:

- Supervising operational activities.
- Monitoring instructor performance.
- Preparing reports.
- Providing technical guidance.
- Supporting disciplinary procedures when necessary.

---

### III — Team Leader

The Team Leader coordinates the instructors within their assigned group or area.

Responsibilities include:

- Coordinating schedules.
- Communicating organizational information.
- Collecting operational information.
- Reporting incidents and accidents involving students.
- Reporting instructor performance.
- Supporting daily operational activities.

---

### II — Advisor

The Advisor provides technical and operational support to instructors and Team Leaders.

Responsibilities include:

- Assisting with problem resolution.
- Providing technical guidance.
- Supporting operational decisions.
- Advising management on administrative and technical matters.
- Helping ensure the program's objectives are achieved.

---

### I — Head Coach

The Head Coach is the instructor's direct supervisor and the first point of contact within the chain of command.

Responsibilities include:

- Supervising instructors at the assigned base.
- Providing technical leadership.
- Mentoring and developing instructors.
- Ensuring training quality and consistency.
- Supporting instructors in operational and technical matters.
- Escalating issues to upper management when necessary.

<Callout type="info">

For operational matters, instructors must always contact their **Head Coach** first and follow the established [chain of command](./chain-of-command).

</Callout>
