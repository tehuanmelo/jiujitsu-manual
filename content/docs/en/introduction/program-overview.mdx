Welcome to the **Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program**.

This manual has been developed to provide instructors with a single reference for the procedures, standards, and responsibilities required to successfully perform their duties across all Palms Sports training bases.

Whether you are a new instructor joining the program or an experienced coach, this manual should be your primary reference for daily operations, administrative procedures, and professional standards.

<Callout type="info">

This manual should be consulted whenever you have questions about operational procedures, testing requirements, documentation, or instructor responsibilities.

</Callout>

## Program Mission

The Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program is committed to providing high-quality instruction through standardized teaching methods, fair student evaluations, professional conduct, and consistent operational procedures across every training base.

Every instructor contributes directly to the development of students and represents both **Palms Sports** and the **Jiu-Jitsu Program**.

---

## Instructor Responsibilities

Every instructor is expected to:

- Teach classes safely and professionally.
- Maintain accurate administrative records.
- Follow all operational procedures.
- Respect the chain of command.
- Maintain a professional appearance.
- Follow the official dress code.
- Complete required reports on time.
- Ensure students meet all testing requirements.
- Promote discipline, respect, and teamwork.

---

## Daily Instructor Workflow

A typical instructor's responsibilities include:

| Step | Responsibility |
| ----- | -------------- |
| 1 | Arrive at the base properly dressed and prepared. |
| 2 | Conduct classes according to program standards. |
| 3 | Record student attendance. |
| 4 | Update student booklets when applicable. |
| 5 | Complete required reports and documentation. |
| 6 | Report injuries or incidents immediately. |
| 7 | Verify testing requirements before signing scorecards. |
| 8 | Follow the chain of command for operational matters. |

## Core Principles

Everything in this manual is based on five fundamental principles.

### Safety

Protect the physical well-being of every student.

### Professionalism

Represent Palms Sports and the Jiu-Jitsu program with integrity and respect.

### Consistency

Apply the same procedures and standards at every training base.

### Accountability

Maintain accurate records and complete all required documentation.

### Leadership

Lead by example and promote discipline, teamwork, and continuous improvement.

## Using This Manual

Each page focuses on one specific procedure.

When performing a task:

1. Read the corresponding section carefully.
2. Follow every required step.
3. Use the provided examples and reference images.
4. When unsure, contact your Head Coach or immediate responsible.

<Callout type="warn">

If a procedure described in this manual conflicts with a direct instruction from management, follow the instruction from your supervisor and report the discrepancy through the chain of command.

</Callout>
