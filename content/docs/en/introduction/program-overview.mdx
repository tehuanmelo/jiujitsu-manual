Welcome to the **Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program**.

This manual was developed to give instructors a single reference for the procedures, standards and responsibilities required to perform their duties successfully at every training base.

Whether you are new to the program or have been teaching in the project for a long time, this manual is your primary reference for daily operations, administrative procedures and professional standards.

<Callout type="info">

This manual should be consulted whenever you have questions about operational procedures, test requirements, documentation or instructor responsibilities.

</Callout>

## Program mission

The Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program is committed to delivering high-quality instruction through standardized teaching methods, fair student evaluations, professional conduct and consistent operational procedures across all training bases.

Every instructor contributes directly to student development and represents both **Palms Sports** and the **Jiu-Jitsu Program**.

---

## Instructor responsibilities

Every instructor must:

- Teach classes safely and professionally.
- Keep accurate administrative records.
- Follow all operational procedures.
- Respect the chain of command.
- Maintain a professional appearance.
- Follow the official dress code.
- Complete the required reports on time.
- Ensure students meet all test requirements.
- Promote discipline, respect and teamwork.

---

## Instructor daily routine

The typical responsibilities of an instructor, in the order of the day:

1. Arrive at the base properly uniformed and prepared.
2. Conduct classes according to program standards.
3. Record student attendance.
4. Stamp the students' booklets.
5. Complete the required reports and documentation.
6. Report injuries or incidents immediately.
7. Verify test requirements before signing scorecards.
8. Follow the chain of command for operational matters.

## Core principles

All the content in this manual is based on five core principles:

| Principle | What it means |
| --------- | ------------- |
| **Safety** | Protect the physical well-being of every student. |
| **Professionalism** | Represent Palms Sports and the Jiu-Jitsu program with integrity and respect. |
| **Consistency** | Apply the same procedures and standards at every training base. |
| **Accountability** | Keep accurate records and complete all required documentation. |
| **Leadership** | Lead by example and promote discipline, teamwork and continuous improvement. |

## How to use this manual

Each page covers a specific procedure.

When performing a task:

1. Read the corresponding section carefully.
2. Follow all required steps.
3. Use the examples and reference images provided.
4. When in doubt, contact your Head Coach or immediate supervisor.

<Callout type="warn">

If a procedure described in this manual conflicts with a direct instruction from management, follow your supervisor's instruction and report the discrepancy through the [chain of command](./chain-of-command).

</Callout>

## Remember

No procedure in this manual exists as a mere formality. Each one serves at least one of these goals:

- **Student safety** — everyone trains and goes home well.
- **Fair evaluations** — the same criteria apply to everyone.
- **Accurate documentation** — what happens in class is recorded.
- **Professional instruction** — the class has a well-run beginning, middle and end.
- **Operational consistency** — the same quality in every group.
- **Shared accountability** — the team moves in the same direction.

By following these standards, you uphold the quality of the Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program and the trust of everyone who trains with us. Good work and good training.
