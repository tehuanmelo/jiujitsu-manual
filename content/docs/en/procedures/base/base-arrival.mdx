Procedure for arriving at the base.

## Entry gate

When arriving at the base by car, at the entry gate, the instructor must:

- Reduce the vehicle's speed.
- Dim the headlights.
- Turn on the vehicle's interior lights, if necessary.
- Turn off the radio.
- Have their **gate pass in hand** for identification.
- Remove any cap or sunglasses they are wearing, for identification.
- Already be following the [Dress code](../../professional-conduct/dress-code#when-entering-the-base) from the moment they arrive at the base.

<Callout type="error">

Electric cars are forbidden from entering the base.

</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">

If the gate security denies entry, the instructor must comply with the order and turn back. The instructor must then contact their superiors, report what happened and wait for instructions on how to proceed.

</Callout>

## Inside the base

From the gate to the training area, if cars are permitted to enter the unit, drive at low speed and keep the radio off.

In the parking area, stop the car only in permitted areas — some spaces are private, so make sure to park in a permitted spot. As a safety standard, the car must always be reverse-parked; always park the same way as the cars already parked there.

If moving around on foot, follow the [Dress code](../../professional-conduct/dress-code#when-entering-the-base).

## Items not permitted inside the base

<Callout type="error">

The use of smartphones, smartwatches, computers, cameras and tablets inside military bases is **strictly forbidden**.

</Callout>
