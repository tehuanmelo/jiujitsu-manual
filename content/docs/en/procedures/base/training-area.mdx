This page sets out the requirements for the training space and the safety check that the instructor must carry out before each class.

## Training area

A suitable space for Jiu-Jitsu practice must be available for the class, with a mat suitable for the sport. The area must be air-conditioned. Water must be available in the training area for the instructor and the students.

## Pre-class check

The training area must be clean, and the mat must be fit for training. Before starting a class, the instructor must always check the condition of the training area, including whether there are gaps between the mat panels that could pose a risk during training.

<Callout type="warn">

Inspect the mat for any sharp objects that could compromise the safety of the instructor or the students.

</Callout>
