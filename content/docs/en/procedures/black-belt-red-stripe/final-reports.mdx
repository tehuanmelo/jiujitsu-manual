title: Final Reports

**How it works:** the final reports are **filled in only after all stages are complete** and must be submitted to **GHQ**.

| Item | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Rule** | Submission of the final documents is **mandatory** |
| **Total** | **2 reports** |

---

### Evaluation & Performance Report – Score Card Black Belt Red Target

Form used to evaluate the student's performance in the test. It records the mark for each evaluated item, the final mark and the result: **PASSED** or **FAILED**.

<div className="mx-auto max-w-sm">

![Evaluation and Performance Report – Score Card Black Belt Red Target form](/black-belt-red-stripe/evaluation-performance-score-card.png)

</div>

**Fields filled in by the unit:** Military Number, Rank, Student's Name in Arabic, Student's Name in English, UAE ID Number, Unit / Sub Unit, Phone N, Birthday, the Medical Fit Exam result and date, Date of the Test and Remarks.

**First Evaluation / Performance Scale:** the form contains **20 items**. Each item receives a rating from the scale below and has the columns **Partial Mark**, **Remark** and **Ps Number**.

| Rating | Mark |
| --- | --- |
| Excellent | **5** |
| V.Good | **4.5** |
| Good | **4** |
| Average | **3** |
| Satisfactory | **2** |
| Poor | **0** |

Items **1 to 13** each correspond to one stage of the program:

1. Completion of the **60 classes** as an assistant coach.
2. Pedagogical methodology.
3. Practical classroom management.
4. Lesson plan methodology (theory).
5. Lesson plan methodology (practice).
6. Class procedure in the army.
7. Motivational physical exercises.
8. Documentation and procedures (attendance, injuries, accidents, etc.).
9. Student evaluation and evaluation models.
10. Management of mixed groups (different belts and stripes).
11. Jiu-Jitsu rules (theory and practice).
12. Baton and knife.
13. Pistol and rifle.

Items **14 to 19** evaluate the student's behavior (Performance Scale):

- **14:** always punctual for activities.
- **15:** always properly dressed.
- **16:** firm, decisive, self-confident and assured.
- **17:** resolute, courageous, not afraid to make unpopular decisions.
- **18:** controlled, calm and predictable.
- **19:** quick and proactive intervention when needed, with good positioning to keep a clear view of the class.

Item **20** is the overall evaluation of the student as Jiu Jitsu Black Belt – Assistant Coach (technical part, technical knowledge and skill).

<Callout type="error">

If the student receives **a mark of zero on any item** of the evaluation, remedial classes and a retake of the test are **mandatory**.

</Callout>

**Final mark:** the total points (Total Marks) determine the final rating (Final Score).

| Final Score | Total Marks |
| --- | --- |
| Excellent | **100–90** |
| V.Good | **89–80** |
| Good | **79–70** |
| Average | **69–60** |
| Satisfactory | **59–41** |
| Poor | **40–0** |

**Signatures:** the form bears the stamp and signature of the person responsible for the test at **GHQ**, as well as the signature blocks of the officer responsible for the unit, the unit stamp, the Technical committee approval and the officers supervising the test.

**Check before signing:**

Before signing and stamping the test form, the responsible officer **must** check, in this order:

1. All fields at the top of the form are filled in and the form was **typed electronically**.
2. The coach's stamp appears on the required classes in the Jiu-Jitsu class record book.
3. The required **120 classes** are recorded as completed in the record book and in the daily attendance record.

### Descriptive Final Report – Black Belt Red Target

Descriptive report of the student's performance in each stage of the program. On the form, the printed title is **Black Belt – Red Target – Final Report**.

<div className="mx-auto max-w-sm">

![Descriptive Final Report – Black Belt Red Target form](/black-belt-red-stripe/descriptive-final-report.png)

</div>

| Filled in by | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| **Student** | Military Number, Rank, Student's Name in Arabic, Student's Name in English, Phone N, UAE ID Number, Unit / Sub Unit, Birthday and Remarks |
| **Coach** | Ps Number, Name of coach, Phone Number, Belt and Strip and Date of the Report |

**Jiu Jitsu – Report:** the coach describes the student's performance in a separate field for each of the **13 items**:

1. Practical lesson (Assistant Coach Course).
2. Pedagogical model (theory).
3. Practical classroom model.
4. Lesson plan methodology (theory).
5. Lesson plan methodology (practice).
6. Understand the army classroom teaching (theory–practice).
7. Jiu Jitsu fitness activities in the armed forces.
8. Documents and procedures.
9. Test models and how to evaluate trainees (theory–practice).
10. Mixed belts/stripes and tournament management (theory–practice).
11. Jiu Jitsu rules seminar review (theory–practice).
12. Baton and knife review (practice).
13. Pistol and rifle review (practice).

**Closing:** the form ends with the **Final Decision** and the **Coach Stamp and Signature**; the latter certifies that the student is ready for the test. These are followed by the signature and stamp blocks of the officer responsible for the unit, the unit stamp, the Technical committee approval and the signatures of the officers supervising the test.
