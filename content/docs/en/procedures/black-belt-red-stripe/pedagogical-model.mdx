---
title: Pedagogical Model
description: Theoretical and practical stage on the pedagogy of the class model.
---

**How it works:** this stage teaches the pedagogy of the class model: the fundamentals, the importance of the movements and the reason each movement is executed.

| Item | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Rule** | Theory and practice **on the same day** |
| **Total** | **5 days**, **10 classes** |
| **Stamp** | **2 stamps per day** (**1** theory + **1** practice) |

<Callout type="info">

**Note:** theory classes are **individual** and last **1 hour**.

</Callout>
