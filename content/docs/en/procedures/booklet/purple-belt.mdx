---
title: Purple Belt
description: What sets the Purple Belt booklet apart and the additional requirements for promotion to Brown Belt.
---

The Purple Belt booklet has **two particularities** compared to the others. In addition to the records common to all belts, it includes a column for the rules test and dedicated pages for baton and knife classes.

<Callout type="warn">

A Purple Belt student **may only be promoted to Brown Belt** after meeting both requirements:

- Passing the **rules test**.
- Completing **10 baton classes** and **10 knife classes**.

</Callout>

---

## Jiu-Jitsu rules test

![Purple Belt Exams page with the rules test stamped](/booklet/purple_rules.png)

Reading the table from right to left, the **Jiu-Jitsu Rules Test** column appears between Stripe 4 and the Brown Belt column. That position **does not indicate order**: as soon as the student becomes a Purple Belt, they may take the rules test, without waiting to complete the stripes.

<Callout type="info">

The rules test can be completed at any point during the Purple Belt, in parallel with the stripes.

What the column determines is **eligibility for promotion**: while it remains blank, the student cannot be promoted to Brown Belt, even if they have already completed all four stripes.

</Callout>

---

## The rules test stamp

The rules test stamp is different from the stripe stamps: in addition to recording the pass, it records **the student's performance on the exam**.

| Information | Example |
| ----------- | ------- |
| Score obtained | 70% |
| Exam date | 18 JUN 2026 |
| Identification | JJC.AR |
| Result | PASS |

The percentage and the identification are written by hand over the stamp, along with the date and the **PASS** marking.

<Callout type="info">

The rules test is listed as a requirement for promotion from Purple Belt to Brown Belt in [Test Requirements](../test/test-requirements).

</Callout>

---

## Baton and knife classes

Promotions for the **White** and **Blue** belts do not require this type of class. A Purple Belt student, however, must complete an additional stage of **20 classes** to be promoted to Brown Belt.

The stage is divided into two blocks, and the booklet includes a grid for each one, with **ten numbered cells**.

![Booklet baton and knife class pages](/booklet/purple_baton_knife.png)

| Grid | Classes required |
| ---- | ---------------- |
| Baton classes | 10 baton classes. |
| Knife classes | 10 knife classes. |

The cells are filled from **right to left**, and the stamp follows the same format as the class record: date, time, instructor's PS and instructor's signature.

<Callout type="info">

Combined, the two grids correspond to the **20 Close Combat classes** required for promotion from Purple Belt to Brown Belt in [Test Requirements](../test/test-requirements).

</Callout>
