---
title: Purple Belt
description: The specific features of the Purple Belt booklet and the additional requirements for promotion to Brown Belt.
---

The Purple Belt booklet has **two specific features** that the other booklets do not have. In addition to the records common to all belts, it has a column for the rules test and separate pages for baton and knife classes.

<Callout type="warn">

A Purple Belt student **can only be promoted to Brown Belt** after meeting both requirements:

- Pass the **rules test**.
- Complete **10 baton classes** and **10 knife classes**.

</Callout>

---

## Jiu-Jitsu rules test

![Purple Belt Jiu-Jitsu exams page with the rules test stamped](/booklet/purple_rules.png)

When the table is read from right to left, the **Jiu-Jitsu Rules Test** column appears between Stripe 4 and the Brown Belt column. This position **does not indicate an order**: as soon as the student becomes a Purple Belt, they may take the rules test. They do not have to wait until they complete the stripes.

<Callout type="info">

The rules test may be completed at any time while the student is a Purple Belt, in parallel with the stripes.

What the column determines is **eligibility for promotion**: while it is blank, the student cannot be promoted to Brown Belt, even if they have already completed all four stripes.

</Callout>

---

## The rules test stamp

The rules test stamp is different from the stripe stamps: in addition to recording the pass, it records **the student's performance on the test**.

| Information        | Example     |
| ------------------ | ----------- |
| Percentage scored  | 70%         |
| Test date          | 18 JUN 2026 |
| Identification     | JJC.AR      |
| Result             | PASS        |

The percentage and the identification are handwritten on the stamp, together with the date and the **PASS** mark.

<Callout type="info">

The rules test is listed as a requirement for promotion from Purple Belt to Brown Belt in [Test requirements](../test/test-requirements).

</Callout>

---

## Baton and knife classes

Promotions from **White** and **Blue** belts do not require this type of class. A Purple Belt student, however, must complete an additional stage of **20 Close Combat classes (10 baton + 10 knife)** to be promoted to Brown Belt.

The stage is divided into two blocks, and the booklet has a grid for each one, with **ten numbered cells**.

![Baton and knife class pages of the booklet](/booklet/purple_baton_knife.png)

| Grid          | Required classes    |
| ------------- | ------------------- |
| Baton classes | 10 baton classes.   |
| Knife classes | 10 knife classes.   |

The cells are filled in from **right to left**, and the stamp follows the same format as the class record: date, time, Instructor's PS and instructor's signature.

<Callout type="info">

Together, the two grids make up the **20 Close Combat classes** required for promotion from Purple Belt to Brown Belt in [Test requirements](../test/test-requirements).

</Callout>
