---
title: Structure setup
description: Procedure and requirements for setting up the physical structure of a unit competition.
---

The competition setup must take place **the day before** the competition. If there are not enough instructors available at the competition venue to carry out the setup, the **Team Leader** or **Advisor** must send instructors from the unit to help with the setup.

The setup covers **6 independent stations**, which do not have to be set up in any particular order: check-in, fight area, podium, awards table, management table and stamp table.

## Check-in

The check-in receives the athletes when they arrive at the competition.

| Item | Quantity per setup |
| --- | --- |
| Table with tablecloth and chair | 1 |
| Computer (plugged in) | 1 |
| Scale to check the athletes' weight | 1 (one per computer) |

The number of check-in setups depends on the amount of equipment provided for the competition and the number of athletes expected.

**Example**: a competition with **5 fight areas** and **500 athletes** expected needs at least **4 check-in setups** — 4 tables with tablecloths and chairs and 4 computers plugged in to receive the athletes and avoid delays at the start of the competition.

## Fight area

Each fight area requires the following equipment.

| Item | Quantity per area |
| --- | --- |
| Table with tablecloth | 1 |
| TV for the scoreboard | 1 |
| Computer to record the score | 1 |
| Red belt to tell the athletes apart | 1 |

<Callout type="warn">

Both the computer and the TV must be connected to power, and the computer must be connected to the TV with an HDMI cable.

</Callout>

## Podium

- The podium must have the **backdrop** provided with the equipment — some units already have the backdrop.
- The podiums provided must be placed in front of the backdrop: **first place in the center**, **second place on the left** and **third place on the right**.

## Awards table

- A table with a **black tablecloth** must be set up next to the podium to hold the medals and trophies.
- The trophies and medals must be arranged on the table for display, grouped by **first**, **second** and **third place**.

## Management table

- A table with a **black tablecloth** for managing the competition must be set up and available.
- The system computer, labeled **"System"** and dedicated to the system, must be available on the table.
- The printer delivered with the equipment must be on the table, connected to the computer, and both must be plugged into the power outlet.
- Clipboards must be laid out on the system table for running the competition.

## Stamp table

- A setup for stamping the students' booklets must be prepared at the competition.
- The table must have a tablecloth and a chair, to receive the students who have already finished competing and stamp their booklets.
