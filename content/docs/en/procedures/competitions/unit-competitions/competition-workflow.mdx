---
title: Competition workflow
description: How the check-in and the fight areas work during a unit competition.
---

The competition follows this order: check-in, fights, awards, stamping.

## Check-in

The check-in uses the [check-in setup](./competition-assembly#check-in) described on the Structure setup page.

The check-in has a set start and end time. At the set start time, the check-in setups must be ready.

One person must be assigned to each setup to carry out the check-in. The Technical Advisor or Team Leader must monitor the process to help with any problems that may come up during the check-in.

To check in, the student must have their ID card, military ID and booklet in hand.

<Callout type="error">

Only students from the unit may take part in the competition. Students from other units cannot take part.

</Callout>

When receiving a student, the person responsible for the check-in must follow this sequence:

1. Collect the student's documents.
2. Fill in all of the student's information.
3. Check the student's weight.

Each setup must have a document listing all the sub-units of the unit running the competition. This is essential to make data entry in the check-in system easier.

After the check-in is complete, the check-in data from each computer must be collected and delivered to the management table so the brackets can be generated.

## Staging area

After the check-in, the student must be sent to the staging area, a predetermined area where the students wait for the competition to start.

## Fight area

Each fight area has **1 Referee**, **1 Bracket**, **1 Mat assistant** and **1 Scoreboard**.

The people for each function must be assigned before the competition starts.

| Function | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Mat assistant** | Call the athletes from the staging area and lead them to the fight area. |
| **Bracket** | Run the fights, follow the bracket sheet and keep the fights on schedule. After each bracket sheet is completed, the Bracket must take the result to the management table. |
| **Referee** | Conduct the fight according to the Jiu-Jitsu rules, making sure the students are in proper uniform and the fight is run correctly. |
| **Scoreboard** | Record the points during the fight and keep watching the Referee so the process runs smoothly. |

## Competition start

After the check-in is finished and the data has been collected and delivered to the management table, the bracket sheets are generated. At the predetermined start time for the fights, the bracket sheets are handed to the **Brackets** so they can start the competition.

All staff must be in position at the set start time.

## Finals

The finals must only be run as defined in the briefing given before the competition starts. In some cases the bracket may be run through to the end; in others, it is not required to wait for the whole competition to end before running the finals.

After the finals, the Bracket must take the bracket sheet with the results to the management table, so the awards ceremony for the athletes can then take place.

## Awards

Once the management table has entered the bracket results into the system, the awards ceremony may take place. A person responsible for this function must be assigned in advance and must follow this sequence:

1. Receive the bracket sheet with the results.
2. Prepare the medals.
3. Call the athletes to the podium.
4. The officer in command of the unit hands the medals to the athletes.

## Stamping

After finishing their participation in the competition, the student must go to the [stamp table](./competition-assembly#stamp-table) to have their booklet stamped as proof of participation.

If the student does not go to the podium, stamping is the next step after the fights. If the student receives an award, stamping takes place after the awards ceremony.

## Closing

The closing starts when the fights are finished and the management table announces the end of the competition.

At that point:

1. The entire competition structure must be taken down.
2. The equipment must go back into its original boxes and containers.
3. The equipment must be placed in the same spot where it was delivered (see [Receiving the equipment](./equipment-receivals)) to wait for pickup by the logistics team.
