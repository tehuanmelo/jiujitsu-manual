---
title: Receiving the equipment
description: Procedure for receiving and checking the equipment for a unit competition.
---

Once the day and time for receiving the equipment are set, **the responsible instructor** must be present on site to receive the equipment.

The procedure below ensures that the equipment received matches what was delivered and that every item is in working condition before the competition setup.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Attendance at equipment receiving

**The responsible instructor** must be present at the agreed location on the set day and time for receiving the equipment. This attendance is mandatory because this person must have **the authority to check the equipment, identify problems and contact the people responsible immediately**, without depending on outside approval when the equipment is received.

</Step>

<Step>

### Checking the equipment against the delivery list

The person delivering the equipment gives the instructor a list. Using this list, the instructor must check that all the equipment received matches the equipment delivered.

</Step>

<Step>

### Reporting missing items

<Callout type="warn">

If any equipment is missing, this must be reported **immediately** to the people responsible.

</Callout>

</Step>

<Step>

### Checking the quality of the items

If all the equipment delivered matches the list, the person who received the equipment must check the quality of each item before moving on.

| Equipment type | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Electronic equipment | Turn it on and test it to confirm that it works. |
| Trophy plates and medals | Confirm that they match the championship being hosted. |

</Step>

<Step>

### Moving to the next stage

If everything checks out, the process moves on to the [competition setup](./competition-assembly).

</Step>

</Steps>
