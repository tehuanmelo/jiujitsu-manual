This page summarizes the instructor's responsibilities in a unit competition, phase by phase. Each section links to the page with the full procedure.

## Receiving the equipment

The equipment is received by the responsible instructor(s). Full procedure in [Receiving the equipment](./equipment-receivals).

- Be at the location on the agreed day and time. Attendance is **mandatory**.
- Check the equipment received against the delivery list.
- Report any missing item to the people in charge **immediately**.
- Test the electronics and check that the trophy plates and medals match the championship.

## Structure setup

The setup takes place **the day before** the competition. Full procedure in [Structure setup](./competition-assembly).

- Set up the **6 stations**: check-in, fight areas, podium, awards table, management table and stamp table.
- Leave the computer and TV of each fight area plugged into power and connected via **HDMI**.
- Prepare enough check-in setups for the expected number of athletes.

## Competition day

Full procedure in [Competition workflow](./competition-workflow).

- Have the check-in setups ready at the start time.
- Perform the assigned function: check-in, Mat assistant, Bracket, Referee, Scoreboard, awards or stamping.
- Be in position at the time the fights start.
- Make sure that **only students from the unit** take part.
- Take the check-in data and the results on the bracket sheets to the management table.

## Closing

Full procedure in [Closing](./competition-workflow#closing).

- Dismantle the structure, return the equipment to its original boxes and containers, and leave the equipment at the place where it was delivered, for collection by the logistics team.
