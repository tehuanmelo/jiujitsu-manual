Every Jiu-Jitsu class must last **80 minutes** and follow the official structure described below. This structure ensures consistency, teaching quality and the same learning experience across all training bases.

Unless instructed otherwise by management, all instructors must follow this sequence.

<Callout type="warn">

All instructors must be on the mat wearing the complete official uniform **at least 10 minutes before the class start time**.

See the [Dress code](../../professional-conduct/dress-code#during-classes).

</Callout>

## The seven phases of the class

### Opening line-up

Before the class starts, the students must be lined up from left to right in order of belt rank. At this point, the instructor welcomes the students and must ask whether any student is injured or has an injury that prevents them from performing a particular movement. The class must begin only after this.

The class is divided into **seven phases**, always in this order:

<Steps>

<Step>

### Joint activation

Prepare the students physically for training by increasing mobility and activating the main joints used during practice.

**Objectives:**

- Increase joint mobility.
- Improve range of motion.
- Reduce the risk of injury.
- Prepare the body for physical activity.

</Step>

<Step>

### Warm up

Raise body temperature and prepare the students for the technical part of the class through dynamic movements.

**Examples include:**

- Light jogging.
- Movement drills.
- Coordination drills.
- Partner warm-up drills.
- Dynamic stretching.

</Step>

<Step>

### Fundamentals

Review and reinforce the fundamental movements of the program.

**Examples include:**

- Breakfalls.
- Rolls.
- Hip escapes.
- Technical stand-up.
- Basic movement drills.

</Step>

<Step>

### Striking and self defense

This phase is dedicated to striking and self defense. It complements Jiu-Jitsu ground training and prepares the students for different scenarios.

<Callout type="info">

The content of this phase depends on the day of the week.

</Callout>

| Day | Content |
| --- | ------- |
| Monday | Striking Training |
| Tuesday | Self Defense |
| Wednesday | Striking Training |
| Thursday | Self Defense |
| Friday | Free Training |
| Saturday | Free Training |
| Sunday | Free Training |

</Step>

<Step>

### Technical development

This phase is dedicated to teaching the **official technique of the day**.

The instructor must follow the technique assigned for that day in the official Jiu-Jitsu Program manual. Techniques must not be replaced or modified unless specifically instructed by management.

So that all students learn at the same pace, the instructor must teach the technique in small sequential steps:

1. Demonstrate the complete technique.
2. Break the technique down into small steps.
3. Explain one step at a time.
4. Have all students perform the same step at the same time.
5. Observe and correct the execution.
6. Move on only after the group has understood the previous step.
7. Finish with the students performing the complete technique.

This method keeps the class synchronized and allows the instructor to supervise each student effectively.

</Step>

<Step>

### Technical drill

The Technical Drill is the sparring phase of the class, in which the students apply the techniques they have learned in a practical combat situation.

Depending on the goal of the class, the instructor may conduct:

- **Specific Training** — Sparring with a defined objective or starting position.
- **Full Sparring** — A complete Jiu-Jitsu match following the official rules.

During this phase, the instructor must:

- Monitor all students continuously.
- Ensure a safe training environment.
- Correct technical mistakes when appropriate.
- Encourage active participation from all students.
- Ensure that all students remain engaged throughout the training session.

</Step>

<Step>

### Cool down

End the class with a proper cooldown that gradually lowers the intensity of physical effort and helps the muscles relax after training.



**Examples include:**

- Light stretching.
- Breathing exercises.

The items below are not part of the cooldown, but they must be done at this point:

- Brief review of the class.
- Questions and answers when appropriate.

</Step>

</Steps>

### Closing line-up

The closing line-up follows the same formation as the opening line-up. At the end of the class, the instructor must give closing remarks with a summary of what was learned, highlighting positive points, such as students who stood out or showed something different, and negative points, such as difficulties observed during the class. It is essential to ask whether any student was injured and whether everyone is feeling well.

---

<Callout type="error">

**Classes shorter than 80 minutes are strictly forbidden.**

Every Jiu-Jitsu class **must** last **80 minutes**. Conducting shorter classes is **completely unacceptable** and is considered a violation of the official program standards.

</Callout>

---

## Student participation

Every student must remain actively engaged throughout the entire class.

Students must **never** stand idle while the others are training.

If necessary, instructors must:

- Organize additional training pairs.
- Adjust group sizes.
- Assign individual drills.
- Rotate students appropriately.

<Callout type="info">

Every student must be practicing or receiving instruction at all times during the class.

</Callout>

---

## Professional communication

Instructors must communicate clearly, respectfully and professionally throughout the class.

During instruction:

- Use simple and objective language.
- Speak clearly and confidently.
- Make constructive corrections.
- Encourage students respectfully.
- Maintain a positive learning environment.
- Avoid unnecessary conversations unrelated to the class.
- Avoid inappropriate or offensive language.

For additional communication standards, see the [Behavior](../../professional-conduct/professional-conduct) and [Chain of command](../../introduction/chain-of-command) sections.

---

## Instructor checklist

During all classes, instructors must:

- Arrive on the mat **at least 10 minutes before** the class time.
- Wear the complete official uniform.
- Follow the official lesson structure.
- Teach the official technique of the day.
- Maintain control of the class at all times.
- Ensure that all students participate actively.
- Correct students respectfully.
- Promote a safe and professional learning environment.

---

## Important

<Callout type="warn">

The official lesson structure is mandatory.

All instructors must follow the class sequence, teach the official curriculum, keep the students actively participating and conduct every class with professionalism and consistency.

</Callout>
