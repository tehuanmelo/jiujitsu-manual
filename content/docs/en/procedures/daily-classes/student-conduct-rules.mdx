---
title: Student conduct
description: Standards for punctuality, behavior and uniform checks required of students during classes.
---

The rules below set the standard of conduct expected of the student during Jiu-Jitsu classes. They cover punctuality, phone use, hygiene, and the uniform and rank checks.

The instructor is responsible for checking these points in every class and for guiding the student whenever something does not meet the required standard.

## Uniform

The official uniform is the army kimono. Kimonos from other companies, teams or clubs are not permitted. Only the kimono of the army or of the unit the student belongs to is allowed.

The kimono is permitted in all classes. In some classes, the uniform may be the military uniform or sportswear; in these cases, the student may attend without the kimono.

## Late arrival grace period

The student has a 15-minute grace period to arrive for class. When a student arrives after this limit, the instructor must politely inform the student of the late arrival grace period rule.

<Callout type="warn">

If the student arrives more than **15 minutes** late, the student may watch the class but does not actually take part, does not receive the class stamp, and does not record their name in the daily attendance.

</Callout>

## Phone use

Phone use is not permitted during class. The student's full attention must be on the training.

## Accessories not permitted

Accessories such as rings, watches, necklaces, bracelets or smart bands are not permitted during class, because they are a safety risk during practice. They must be removed before the student takes part in training.

## Hygiene

In every class, the instructor must check that the student has trimmed nails and that the kimono issued by the base is clean and in good condition. Personal hygiene and a kimono in good condition are the Palms Sports and army standard of excellence.

<Callout type="info">

Pay particular attention to long nails: they can injure both the student and their training partners.

</Callout>

## Belt and rank check

The instructor must check that the student's belt matches the rank recorded in the booklet. This information can be checked in the [student's booklet](../booklet/booklet#jiu-jitsu-exams). If the student's belt is different, that belt must not be worn during class.

## Guidance when something does not meet the standard

<Callout type="info">

If something does not meet the standard required by the army, the instructor must be polite and only guide the student on the correct standard, without reprimanding or embarrassing them.

</Callout>
