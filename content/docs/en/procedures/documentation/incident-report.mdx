---

Complete an Incident Report whenever an unusual event or an operational problem occurs during the Jiu-Jitsu Program. Reporting keeps records accurate, supports improvements in operations and informs management of situations that could affect classes, instructors, students or facilities.

<Callout type="warn">

Every operational incident must be reported **as soon as possible**. Do not wait until the end of the day to complete the report.

</Callout>

## When an Incident Report is required

The instructor must complete an Incident Report whenever an unusual event affects the normal operation of the program.

Examples include:

- Problems with the training facility.
- Unexpected inspections.
- Unexpected class cancellations.
- Equipment damage or malfunction.
- Safety issues inside the training area.
- Problems that affect the scheduled class.
- Security-related incidents.
- Any operational event that is unusual or that management needs to know about.

<Callout type="info">

If an event is unusual, affects normal operations or may need to be referred to later, complete an Incident Report.

</Callout>

---

## Reporting procedure

Follow the steps below whenever an operational incident occurs.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Manage the situation

- Ensure the safety of students and instructors.
- Take the immediate actions required to reduce the impact of the incident.
- Inform the appropriate military or base personnel, if required.
- Continue or suspend the class, as the situation requires.

</Step>

<Step>

### Complete the Incident Report

Complete the Incident Report as soon as possible after the event.

<div className="mx-auto max-w-sm">

![Incident Report form](/incident-report.png)

</div>

The report must include:

- Date and time of the incident.
- Training base.
- Instructor responsible for the class.
- Description of the incident.
- People involved.
- Actions taken.
- Current status.

</Step>

<Step>

### Submit the report

After completing the report:

- Hand the original Incident Report to the base command.
- Keep a copy in the Jiu-Jitsu room for record-keeping purposes.
- Complete and submit the online Incident Report.

</Step>
</Steps>

**All three steps are mandatory.**

---

## Examples of reportable incidents

All of the situations below require an Incident Report:

- Unexpected inspection of the facility.
- Training area unavailable.
- Class canceled unexpectedly.
- Damaged mats or training equipment.
- Air conditioning or lighting failure.
- Water leak or damage to the facility.
- Security problem affecting the class.
- Any unusual operational event.

---

## Instructor responsibility

The instructor conducting the class is responsible for ensuring that:

- The Incident Report is completed correctly.
- The report is submitted without unnecessary delay.
- The base command receives the original report.
- A copy is stored in the Jiu-Jitsu room.
- The online Incident Report is completed and submitted.

Failure to report operational incidents can affect safety, administrative records and the management of the Jiu-Jitsu Program.

---

## Incident Report vs. Injury Report

| Situation | Report type |
| ---------- | ----------- |
| Student injury or physical accident | [Injury Report](./injury-report) |
| Operational problem or unusual event | **Incident Report** |

<Callout type="info">

If an incident at the facility results in a student injury, **both an Incident Report and an Injury Report may be required**, depending on the circumstances.

</Callout>

---

## Important

<Callout type="error">

Every unusual operational event that affects the Jiu-Jitsu Program must be documented.

When in doubt, complete an Incident Report and notify your immediate superior.

Never leave an operational incident undocumented.

</Callout>
