An Injury Report is required whenever a student is injured during a Jiu-Jitsu class. Prompt and accurate reporting ensures that the incident is properly documented and that the appropriate personnel are informed.

<Callout type="warn">

Every injury must be reported **immediately**. Do not wait until the end of the day or the next class.

</Callout>

## When an Injury Report Is Required

An Injury Report must be completed whenever:

- A student is injured during a Jiu-Jitsu class.
- A student reports pain or discomfort after an incident.
- Any accident occurs during training, regardless of its severity.
- An incident occurs that could potentially result in an injury, even if no visible injury is present.

<Callout type="info">

When in doubt, **complete the Injury Report**. It is always better to document an incident than to leave it unreported.

</Callout>

---

## Reporting Procedure

If an injury or incident occurs, the instructor must complete the following steps immediately.

<Steps>
  <Step>

  ### Assist the Student

  - Stop the activity if necessary.
  - Ensure the student receives appropriate assistance.
  - Follow the base's emergency procedures if medical attention is required.

  </Step>

  <Step>

  ### Complete the Injury Report

  Complete the Injury Report form as soon as possible after the incident.

  The report should include all relevant information about:

  - The student involved.
  - The date and time of the incident.
  - The location.
  - A clear description of what happened.
  - Any immediate actions taken.

  </Step>

  <Step>

  ### Submit the Report

  After completing the report:

  - Submit the original Injury Report to the base command.
  - Store one copy in the Jiu-Jitsu room for record keeping.
  - Complete and submit the online Injury Report.

  </Step>
</Steps>

All three steps are mandatory.

---

## Accident Reporting

An Injury Report is required even if the incident appears to be minor.

Examples include:

- A student falls but continues training.
- A student twists an ankle but reports only minor pain.
- A student receives a light impact and initially appears to be fine.
- Any unusual event that may later develop into an injury.

<Callout type="warn">

Do **not** decide that an incident is "too minor" to report.

If an incident occurs during class, the reporting procedure must be completed according to program policy.

</Callout>

---

## Instructor Responsibility

The instructor conducting the class is responsible for ensuring that:

- The Injury Report is completed accurately.
- The report is submitted immediately.
- The base command receives the original report.
- A copy is stored in the Jiu-Jitsu room.
- The online Injury Report is completed and submitted.

<Callout type="error">

Failure to report an injury or incident may compromise student safety, administrative records, and compliance with program procedures.

</Callout>
---

## Important

<Callout type="warn">

Every injury, or accident that occurs during a Jiu-Jitsu class must be reported.

If there is any doubt about whether an Injury Report is required, **complete the report**.

Never leave an incident undocumented.

</Callout>
