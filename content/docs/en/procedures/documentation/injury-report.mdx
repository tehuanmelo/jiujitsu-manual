title: Injury Report

---

An Injury Report is required whenever a student is injured during a Jiu-Jitsu class. Fast and accurate reporting ensures that the incident is documented and that the right people are informed.

<Callout type="warn">

Every injury must be reported **immediately**. Do not wait until the end of the day or until the next class.

</Callout>

## When an Injury Report Is Required

The instructor must complete an Injury Report whenever:

- A student is injured during a Jiu-Jitsu class.
- A student reports pain or discomfort after an incident.
- Any accident occurs during training, regardless of severity.
- An incident occurs that could result in injury, even without a visible injury.

<Callout type="info">

When in doubt, **complete the Injury Report**. It is always better to document an incident than to leave it unrecorded.

</Callout>

---

## Reporting Procedure

If an injury or incident occurs, the instructor must immediately complete the following steps.

<Steps>
  <Step>

  ### Assist the Student

  - Stop the activity, if necessary.
  - Ensure the student receives proper assistance.
  - Follow the base emergency procedures if medical care is needed.

  </Step>

  <Step>

  ### Complete the Injury Report

  Complete the Injury Report form as soon as possible after the incident.

  <div className="mx-auto max-w-sm">

  ![Injury Report form](/injury-report.png)

  </div>

  The report must include all relevant information about:

  - The student involved.
  - The date and time of the incident.
  - The location.
  - A clear description of what happened.
  - Any immediate actions taken.

  </Step>

  <Step>

  ### Submit the Report

  After completing the report:

  - Deliver the original Injury Report to the base command.
  - Keep a copy in the Jiu-Jitsu room for record keeping.
  - Complete and submit the online Injury Report.

  </Step>
</Steps>

**All three steps are mandatory.**

---

## Accident Reporting

An Injury Report is required even if the incident seems minor.

Examples include:

- A student falls but continues training.
- A student twists an ankle but reports only mild pain.
- A student takes a light impact and initially appears to be fine.
- Any unusual event that could develop into an injury later.

<Callout type="warn">

**Do not** decide that an incident is "too minor" to be reported.

If an incident occurs during class, complete the reporting procedure according to program policy.

</Callout>

---

## Instructor Responsibility

The instructor conducting the class is responsible for ensuring that:

- The Injury Report is completed correctly.
- The report is submitted immediately.
- The base command receives the original report.
- A copy is stored in the Jiu-Jitsu room.
- The online Injury Report is completed and submitted.

<Callout type="error">

Failing to report an injury or incident can compromise student safety, administrative records and compliance with program procedures.

</Callout>

---

## Important

<Callout type="warn">

Every injury or accident that occurs during a Jiu-Jitsu class must be reported.

If there is any doubt about whether an Injury Report is needed, **complete the report**.

Never leave an incident undocumented.

</Callout>
