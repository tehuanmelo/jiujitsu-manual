---
title: Test requirements
description: Minimum requirements a student must meet before taking a belt or stripe promotion test.
---

Before approving a student for a test, the instructor must confirm that the student has completed all the classes, competitions and assessments required for that test.

<Callout type="warn">

A student is **not eligible** for a promotion test until **all minimum requirements** have been completed and verified.

</Callout>

## Requirements by belt level

<Tabs items={['White Belt', 'Blue Belt', 'Purple Belt', 'Brown Belt']}>

<Tab value="White Belt">

| Promotion                   | Minimum requirements                                                       |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Stripe 1**                | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes                                                       |
| **Stripe 2**                | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes                                                       |
| **Stripe 3**                | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes                                                       |
| **Stripe 4**                | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes                                                       |
| **White Belt → Blue Belt**  | • 3 Jiu-Jitsu classes (revision)<br />• Participation in **2 competitions** |

</Tab>

<Tab value="Blue Belt">

| Promotion                   | Minimum requirements                                                       |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| **Stripe 2**                | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes                                                       |
| **Stripe 3**                | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes                                                       |
| **Stripe 4**                | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes                                                       |
| **Blue Belt → Purple Belt** | • 3 Jiu-Jitsu classes (revision)<br />• Participation in **2 competitions** |

</Tab>

<Tab value="Purple Belt">

| Promotion                    | Minimum requirements                                                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| **Stripe 2**                 | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes                                                                                                                  |
| **Stripe 3**                 | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes                                                                                                                  |

</Tabs>

## Instructor responsibility

<Callout type="info">

Before signing or approving a promotion test, the instructor must confirm that the student has successfully completed **all required items** for that promotion.

</Callout>

## Important

<Callout type="warn">

Authorizing a promotion test for a student who does not meet the minimum requirements compromises the integrity of the evaluation process.

</Callout>
