---
title: Dress code
description: Dress requirements for instructors at training bases and at competitions.
---

All instructors must follow the official dress code, keep a professional appearance and represent the Jiu-Jitsu Program properly at all times.

This page covers **two scenarios**: dress at the training base and dress at competitions.

## Training base

### When entering the base

When entering any training base, facility or building, instructors must wear appropriate sportswear.

- Sportswear is mandatory.
- Long pants and closed-toe shoes are mandatory.
- Adidas sportswear is preferred whenever possible.

<Callout type="error">

Shorts and flip-flops are **not** permitted when moving around the base.

</Callout>

### Areas where shorts are permitted

Shorts may be worn only during sports activities, in areas such as:

- Gym
- Sports field
- Running tracks

<Callout type="warn">

Outside these areas, instructors must wear long pants.

</Callout>

### During classes

While teaching, instructors must wear the company's full official uniform. All items are **mandatory**:

- The company's official Jiu-Jitsu kimono (gi).
- The company's official rash guard.
- A belt in good condition that matches the instructor's rank.
- A tag on the kimono with the instructor's name and PS number.

### Footwear during classes

Flip-flops are permitted only inside the Jiu-Jitsu training area. The instructor may also wear them when moving around the facility before or after training.

Outside the Jiu-Jitsu facility, instructors must wear closed-toe shoes.

---

## Competitions

At competitions there are **two outfits**, both official: the regular staff outfit and the referee outfit. The outfit is determined by the function the instructor performs at the event.

Both are **formal outfits**, and they must consist of a **long-sleeve dress shirt** and **dress pants**. The difference is in the shirt and the tie. Pants, socks, dress belt and shoes are black and follow the same standard in both outfits.

<Tabs items={['Staff', 'Referee']}>

<Tab value="Staff">

The regular staff outfit is **all black**:

- Black long-sleeve dress shirt
- Black dress pants
- Black socks
- Black dress belt
- Black closed-toe dress shoes

</Tab>

<Tab value="Referee">

The referee outfit is distinguished by the **white shirt** and the **black tie**:

- White long-sleeve dress shirt
- Black tie
- Black dress pants
- Black socks
- Black dress belt
- Black closed-toe dress shoes

</Tab>

</Tabs>

<Callout type="error">

The following are **not permitted** in either outfit: short-sleeve shirts, athletic pants or athletic sneakers.

</Callout>
