---
title: Dress Code
description: Clothing requirements for instructors across all base facilities.
---

All instructors must follow the official dress code to maintain a professional appearance and represent the Jiu-Jitsu Program at all times.

## When Entering the Base

When entering any training base, facility, or premises, instructors must wear appropriate athletic clothing.

- Athletic clothing is mandatory.
- Long pants and closed shoes are mandatory.
- Adidas athletic clothing is preferred whenever possible.

<Callout type="error">

Shorts and flip-flops are **not** permitted when moving around the base.

</Callout>

---

## Areas Where Shorts Are Permitted

Shorts may only be worn during sports activities, in areas such as:

- Gym
- Sports Field
- Running Tracks

<Callout type="warn">

Outside these areas, instructors must wear long pants.

</Callout>

---

## During Classes

While teaching, instructors must wear the complete official company uniform. All items are **mandatory**:

- Official company Jiu-Jitsu kimono (gi).
- Official company rash guard.
- Belt matching the instructor's rank.
- Company badge with the instructor's name and PS number.

---

## Footwear During Classes

Flip-flops are permitted only inside the Jiu-Jitsu training area. The instructor may also wear them when moving around the facility before or after training.

Outside the Jiu-Jitsu facility, instructors must wear closed shoes.
