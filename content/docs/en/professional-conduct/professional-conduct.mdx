---
title: Behavior
description: Behavior and professional conduct standards for all Jiu-Jitsu instructors.
---

Before teaching or representing the Jiu-Jitsu Program, every instructor must maintain the highest standards of professionalism, discipline, and respect, because their conduct represents both Palms Sports and the program itself.

The standards described in this document apply at all times while on duty, including classes, tests, competitions, meetings, and whenever the instructor is inside any training facility.

<Callout type="warn">

Professional conduct is a fundamental responsibility of every instructor. Failure to meet these standards may result in disciplinary action.

</Callout>

## Professional behavior

Every instructor must:

- Treat all students and colleagues with respect.
- Maintain a professional attitude at all times.
- Communicate in a respectful, calm, and courteous manner.
- Be patient and willing to answer questions.
- Promote a positive, safe, and welcoming learning environment.
- Demonstrate discipline, integrity, and professionalism through their actions.
- Lead by example both inside and outside the training area.

---

## Relationship with students

The instructor's main responsibility is to support student development.

Therefore, every instructor must:

- Listen to students attentively.
- Be approachable and available to help.
- Encourage learning through positive instruction.
- Correct mistakes respectfully.
- Treat all students fairly and equally.
- Maintain a professional relationship with all students.

<Callout type="info">

Students must always feel respected, supported, and comfortable asking questions during training.

</Callout>

### Professional boundaries

The relationship between instructor and student is **strictly professional** and must remain limited to the training environment and the program's official activities.

Being approachable and courteous is part of the job. Building personal bonds is not.

The following is not permitted:

- Developing friendships or personal relationships with students.
- Discussing religion or politics with students.
- Meeting students outside the work environment, including lunches, dinners, outings, or private events.
- Visiting a student's residence or receiving students at the instructor's residence.
- Keeping in contact with students through personal channels for matters unrelated to training.
- Engaging in any form of social life with students outside the program's official activities.

<Callout type="warn">

All communication and interaction with students must take place within the training environment and through official channels. Personal meetings while off duty are **forbidden**.

If a student proposes personal contact, the instructor must decline respectfully and inform their Head Coach.

</Callout>

---

## Personal appearance

Instructors must maintain a clean and professional appearance.

### Personal presentation standards

| Requirement | Standard |
| ------------ | -------- |
| Hair | Clean, tidy, and well groomed |
| Beard | Clean, trimmed, and well groomed |
| Personal hygiene | Must be maintained at all times |
| Uniform | Clean and in good condition |

### Tattoos

Visible tattoos are **not permitted** while on duty.

<Callout type="warn">

Any tattoo must remain completely covered at all times: during classes, meetings, competitions, and whenever the instructor is inside National Guard facilities.

</Callout>

---

## Dress code

Every instructor must follow the official dress code while on duty.

The requirements include appropriate sportswear when entering the base, the official company uniform during classes, and compliance with all appearance standards.

See [Dress code](./dress-code) for all requirements.

---

## Working with colleagues

Professional teamwork is essential to the success of the Jiu-Jitsu Program.

Every instructor must:

- Treat colleagues with respect.
- Cooperate with other instructors.
- Share knowledge and help colleagues whenever possible.
- Maintain a positive work environment.
- Resolve disagreements professionally.
- Support new instructors when they need guidance.
- Work together to achieve the program's goals.

---

## Following instructions

Instructors must follow all legitimate instructions issued by their supervisors.

This includes:

- Following operating procedures.
- Completing assigned tasks on time.
- Respecting schedules and plans.
- Supporting the program's official decisions.
- Following Palms Sports policies.

If any clarification is required, instructors must respectfully ask their supervisor before acting.

---

## Professional development

Every instructor must maintain effective communication in English and stay up to date on the rules and technical evolution of Jiu-Jitsu.

See [Professional standards](../professional-development/professional-development) for all requirements.

---

## Professional ethics

Every instructor must:

- Act with honesty and integrity.
- Protect confidential information.
- Avoid behavior that could harm the program's reputation.
- Never misuse their position or authority.
- Remain impartial and professional when making decisions.
- Set a positive example for students and colleagues.

<Callout type="error">

Sharing students' military numbers or any confidential information with third parties is forbidden.

</Callout>

---

## Prohibited conduct

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Disrespectful language or behavior.
- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Aggressive or inappropriate communication.
- Ignoring supervisors' instructions.
- Bypassing the chain of command without authorization.
- Creating conflicts between colleagues.
- Building friendships or personal social ties with students outside the training environment.
- Wearing inappropriate clothing while on duty.
- Failing to maintain professional appearance standards.
- Leaving visible tattoos uncovered.
- Sharing students' military numbers or any confidential information.
- Any conduct that harms the image of the Jiu-Jitsu Program or Palms Sports.
