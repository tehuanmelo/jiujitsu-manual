title: Behavior
description: Behavior and professional conduct standards for all Jiu-Jitsu instructors.
---

Before teaching or representing the Jiu-Jitsu Program, every instructor must uphold the highest standards of professionalism, discipline, and respect. Instructors represent not only themselves, but also Palms Sports and the Jiu-Jitsu Program.

The standards described in this document apply at all times while on duty, including classes, tests, competitions, meetings, and whenever inside any training facility.

<Callout type="warn">

Professional conduct is a fundamental responsibility of every instructor. Failure to meet these standards may result in disciplinary action.

</Callout>

## Professional behavior

Every instructor must:

- Treat all students and colleagues with respect.
- Maintain a professional attitude at all times.
- Communicate in a respectful, calm, and courteous manner.
- Be patient and willing to answer questions.
- Create a positive, safe, and welcoming learning environment.
- Demonstrate discipline, integrity, and professionalism through their actions.
- Lead by example on and off the training area.

---

## Relationship with students

The instructor's primary responsibility is to support student development.

Every instructor must:

- Listen to students attentively.
- Be approachable and available to help.
- Encourage learning through positive instruction.
- Correct mistakes with respect.
- Treat all students fairly and equally.
- Maintain a professional relationship with all students.

<Callout type="info">

Students must always feel respected, supported, and comfortable asking questions during training.

</Callout>

### Professional boundaries

The relationship between instructor and student is **strictly professional** and must remain limited to the training environment and the program's official activities.

Being approachable and courteous is part of the job. Building personal bonds is not.

It is not permitted to:

- Develop friendships or personal relationships with students.
- Meet students outside the work environment, including lunches, dinners, outings, or private events.
- Visit a student's residence or receive students at your residence.
- Keep in contact with students through personal channels for matters unrelated to training.
- Establish any form of social interaction outside the program's official activities.

<Callout type="warn">

All communication and interaction with students must take place within the training environment and through official channels. Personal meetings outside of duty are **prohibited**.

If a student takes the initiative to propose personal contact, the instructor must respectfully decline and inform their Head Coach.

</Callout>

---

## Personal appearance

Instructors must maintain a clean and professional appearance.

### Personal presentation standards

| Requirement | Standard |
| ------------ | -------- |
| Hair | Clean, neat, and well groomed |
| Beard | Clean, trimmed, and well groomed |
| Personal hygiene | Must be maintained at all times |
| Uniform | Clean and in good condition |

### Tattoos

Visible tattoos are **not permitted** while on duty.

<Callout type="warn">

Any tattoo must remain completely covered at all times: classes, meetings, competitions, and whenever the instructor is inside National Guard facilities.

</Callout>

---

## Dress code

Every instructor must follow the official dress code while on duty.

Requirements include appropriate athletic clothing when entering the base, the official company uniform during classes, and compliance with all appearance standards.

See the [Dress Code](./dress-code) for the full requirements.

---

## Working with colleagues

Professional teamwork is essential to the success of the Jiu-Jitsu Program.

Every instructor must:

- Treat colleagues with respect.
- Cooperate with fellow instructors.
- Share knowledge and help colleagues whenever possible.
- Maintain a positive work environment.
- Resolve disagreements professionally.
- Support new instructors when they need guidance.
- Work together to achieve the program's goals.

---

## Following instructions

Instructors must follow all legitimate instructions issued by their supervisors.

This includes:

- Following operational procedures.
- Completing assigned tasks on time.
- Respecting schedules and planning.
- Supporting the program's official decisions.
- Following Palms Sports policies.

If clarification is needed, instructors must respectfully ask their supervisor before acting.

---

## Professional development

Every instructor must maintain effective communication in English and stay current with the rules and technical evolution of Jiu-Jitsu.

See [Professional Standards](../professional-development/professional-development) for the full requirements.

---

## Professional ethics

Every instructor must:

- Act with honesty and integrity.
- Protect confidential information.
- Avoid behavior that could harm the program's reputation.
- Never misuse their position or authority.
- Remain impartial and professional when making decisions.
- Set a positive example for students and colleagues.

---

## Prohibited conduct

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Disrespectful language or behavior.
- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Aggressive or inappropriate communication.
- Ignoring instructions from supervisors.
- Bypassing the chain of command without authorization.
- Creating conflict among colleagues.
- Building friendships or personal social ties with students outside the training environment.
- Wearing inappropriate clothing while on duty.
- Failing to maintain professional appearance standards.
- Leaving visible tattoos uncovered.
- Any conduct that damages the image of the Jiu-Jitsu Program or Palms Sports.

---

## Instructor commitment

By serving as an instructor, you commit to:

- Upholding the highest standards of professionalism.
- Treating everyone with respect.
- Following the chain of command.
- Working collaboratively with your colleagues.
- Following the official dress code.
- Pursuing continuous improvement in the English language and technical knowledge.
- Supporting the mission and values of the Jiu-Jitsu Program.
- Representing Palms Sports with integrity at all times.
