title: Annual evaluation

All Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu instructors go through an annual evaluation. It verifies the competencies required for the role and covers three main aspects: teaching methodology, communication in English and physical conditioning.

<Callout type="info">

The annual evaluation is mandatory for all instructors, regardless of how long they have worked in the program.

</Callout>

## Purpose

The annual evaluation is intended to:

- Verify the instructor's command of the program and the teaching methodology.
- Confirm that the official class structure is being followed.
- Measure the level of spoken and written English.
- Assess the instructor's physical condition.
- Identify areas for improvement and guide the development of each instructor.

---

## What is evaluated

| Area | What is evaluated | Format |
| ---- | ---------------- | ------- |
| Program and methodology | Command of the content, class structure and official teaching methodology. | Practical class |
| Spoken English | Ability to instruct clearly in English. | Practical class |
| Written English | Comprehension and writing in English. | Written test |
| Physical condition | Conditioning and physical fitness for the role. | Physical test and body measurements |

---

## Evaluation stages

<Steps>
  <Step>

  ### Body measurements

  The instructor's body measurements are recorded as part of the physical condition assessment. This record makes it possible to check whether the instructor meets the fitness standard required for the role and to track their progress over the years.

  </Step>

  <Step>

  ### Practical class

  The instructor leads a complete class, evaluated by a designated member of technical management.

  The evaluation covers:

  - Command of the program content.
  - Compliance with the official class structure.
  - Application of the teaching methodology of the Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program.
  - Clarity and correctness of spoken English during instruction.
  - Management of the group, and the organization and safety of the class.

  <Callout type="info">

  The practical class evaluates alignment with the program and the level of spoken English at the same time, because both reflect the instructor's actual performance on duty.

  </Callout>

  </Step>

  <Step>

  ### Physical test

  The instructor takes a physical test that assesses their conditioning and their fitness to perform their duties in service. See how it works in [Physical test](./physical-test).

  </Step>

  <Step>

  ### Written English test

  The written test assesses the instructor's written English: comprehension and the ability to write clearly and correctly.

  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Result

The evaluation result is communicated to the instructor through the chain of command.

When any area falls below the required standard, the instructor receives guidance from their **Superior** on the expected improvement measures, with follow-up until the next evaluation.

<Callout type="warn">

The annual evaluation is not an isolated event. It verifies what is expected of the instructor throughout the whole year. For this reason, preparation must be continuous, not left until the evaluation period.

</Callout>
