---
title: Annual evaluation
description: Annual evaluation of instructors in program knowledge, teaching methodology, English, and physical condition.
---

All instructors of the Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program are evaluated **annually**.

The evaluation verifies that the instructor maintains the competencies required by the role and works in alignment with the official curriculum.

<Callout type="info">

The annual evaluation is mandatory for all instructors, regardless of how long they have been in the program.

</Callout>

## Purpose

The annual evaluation is intended to:

- Verify the instructor's command of the curriculum and the teaching methodology.
- Confirm that the official lesson structure is being followed.
- Measure the level of spoken and written English.
- Assess the instructor's physical condition.
- Identify areas for improvement and guide each instructor's development.

---

## What is evaluated

| Area | What is evaluated | Format |
| ---- | ---------------- | ------- |
| Curriculum and Methodology | Command of the content, lesson structure, and official teaching methodology. | Practical class |
| Spoken English | Clarity, correctness, and ability to instruct in English. | Practical class |
| Written English | Comprehension and written production in English. | Written exam |
| Physical Condition | Conditioning and physical fitness for the role. | Physical test and body measurements |

---

## Evaluation stages

<Steps>
  <Step>

  ### Body measurements

  The instructor's body measurements are recorded as part of the physical condition assessment. The record verifies that the instructor meets the fitness standard required by the role and tracks their progress over the years.

  </Step>

  <Step>

  ### Practical class

  The instructor leads a complete class, evaluated by a designated member of technical management.

  The following are evaluated:

  - Command of the curriculum content.
  - Compliance with the official lesson structure.
  - Application of the Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program teaching methodology.
  - Clarity and correctness of spoken English during instruction.
  - Class management, organization, and safety.

  <Callout type="info">

  The practical class evaluates alignment with the curriculum and the level of spoken English at the same time, as both reflect the instructor's actual performance on duty.

  </Callout>

  </Step>

  <Step>

  ### Physical test

  The instructor takes a physical test that assesses their conditioning and fitness to perform their duties on the job.

  </Step>

  <Step>

  ### Written English exam

  The written exam assesses the instructor's English in written form: comprehension and the ability to produce clear and correct communication.

  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Results

The evaluation result is communicated to the instructor through the chain of command.

If any area falls below the required standard, the instructor will receive guidance from their **Superior** on the expected improvement measures, with follow-up until the next evaluation.

<Callout type="warn">

The annual evaluation is not an isolated event. It verifies what is expected of the instructor throughout the entire year — preparation must therefore be continuous, not concentrated in the evaluation period.

</Callout>

---

## Preparation

The instructor stays prepared for the annual evaluation by meeting, day to day, the requirements described in [Professional Standards](./professional-development):

- English communication, spoken and written.
- Knowledge of the curriculum and the teaching methodology.
- Currency in technique and rules.
- Physical fitness.

<Callout type="info">

The dates, criteria, and parameters of each stage are defined by technical management and communicated in advance through the chain of command.

</Callout>
