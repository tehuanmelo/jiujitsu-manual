The physical test for Black Belt instructors follows the official **Conditioning check Black Belt** form. It measures the instructor's conditioning by having them perform **3 Jiu-Jitsu techniques** in succession within a fixed time.

The form lists the units **G.H.Q**, **Presidential Guard** and **NSA**, and is identified by the fields **Name**, **PS Number** and **Base**.

---

## How it works

Each technique is performed for **20 seconds**, followed by **20 seconds** of isometric rest. The sequence of the 3 techniques makes up one set.

- The test has **3 repetitions** (sets).
- The total time is **6 minutes**.

---

## Techniques

The techniques are performed with a partner, in the order below.

| Technique | Starting position and execution | Isometric rest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Sprawl followed by double leg | Start standing, facing the partner. Apply the sprawl and finish in the double leg position. | From closed guard |
| 2. Kimura from guard | Start in open guard. Perform the kimura on both sides. | Skydiver position |
| 3. Knee on belly | Start in knee on belly. Apply the knee on belly on both sides. | Partner row |

---

## Scoring

The Individual Test Score compares the performance in sets 2 and 3 with the performance in the first set:

**Individual Test Score = ((set 2 repetitions + set 3 repetitions) ÷ 2) ÷ set 1 repetitions**

In other words, take the average number of repetitions in sets 2 and 3, then divide the result by the number of repetitions in set 1.

---

## Categories

The Individual Test Score places the instructor in one of the SFTI Categories below.

| Score | Category |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 0.3 | Unsatisfactory |
| 0.31 to 0.5 | Poor |
| 0.51 to 0.8 | Average |
| 0.81 to 1.0 | Good |
| 1.1 or more | Excellent |

## Minimum limit of the first set by age

The number of repetitions in the **first set** must reach the minimum defined for the instructor's age group.

| Age | Sprawl/Takedown | Kimura Sit-up | Knee on belly | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20–24 | 11 | 16 | 14 | 41 |
| 25–29 | 10 | 15 | 13 | 38 |
| 30–34 | 9 | 14 | 12 | 35 |
| 35–39 | 8 | 13 | 11 | 32 |
| 40–44 | 7 | 12 | 10 | 29 |
| 45–49 | 6 | 11 | 9 | 26 |
| 50–54 | 5 | 10 | 8 | 23 |
| 55–59 | 4 | 9 | 7 | 20 |
| 60–64 | 3 | 8 | 6 | 17 |
