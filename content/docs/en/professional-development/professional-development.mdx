title: Professional standards

Maintaining and developing the competencies required by the role is the responsibility of every instructor.

The Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program expects its instructors to improve continuously on three fronts: **English communication**, **technical currency** — including the curriculum and the teaching methodology — and **physical fitness**. All of them directly impact the quality of instruction, student safety, and the program's image.

<Callout type="info">

Professional development is not a one-time requirement. It is a permanent commitment, monitored throughout the instructor's time in the program.

</Callout>

## English communication

English is the working language at the training bases. It is the language in which the instructor conducts the class, guides students, completes documentation, and communicates with management.

### Requirements

Every instructor must:

- Conduct the class and communicate with students in English.
- Understand instructions, announcements, and guidance received in English.
- Produce clear and correct written communication in English in reports and official channels.
- Actively pursue improvement of the language — spoken and written — if their current level is not sufficient for the role.

### Continuous improvement

If the instructor's English level does not yet fully meet the demands of the role, the instructor must maintain an active learning process through:

- Self-study and daily practice.
- Language courses or programs.
- Using English in everyday communication with colleagues and students.

<Callout type="warn">

Limited command of English is not accepted as a permanent condition. Instructors are expected to make a continuous and visible effort to improve.

</Callout>

---

## Technical currency

Jiu-Jitsu evolves constantly, as do the rules that govern practice and competition. Keeping up with this evolution is part of the instructor's role.

### Requirements

Every instructor must:

- Follow the official Jiu-Jitsu rules and their updates.
- Stay informed about the sport's technical evolution.
- Know the official Palms Sports curriculum and follow its updates.
- Master and apply the teaching methodology adopted by the program, incorporating published revisions.
- Sustain their own training and technical level over time.
- Follow the announcements and updates published by the program.
- Share relevant rule or methodology updates with the team.

### Areas to follow

| Area | What to follow |
| ---- | ---------------- |
| Rules | Official regulations, scoring changes, fouls, and refereeing criteria. |
| Technique | Evolution of positions, systems, and trends applied to competition and training. |
| Methodology | Teaching methods, instructional progression, and adaptation to student level. |
| Curriculum | Internal announcements, procedure updates, and manual standards. |

<Callout type="info">

Staying current is not a differentiator — it is an essential part of the role. It sustains the instructor's technical authority before the students, raises the standard of the entire team, and safeguards the credibility of the Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program before the training bases.

</Callout>

---

## Physical fitness

The instructor leads classes, demonstrates techniques, and trains alongside students. Maintaining good physical condition is part of the role and supports both the quality of instruction and the example set for students.

### Requirements

Every instructor must:

- Maintain physical conditioning compatible with leading classes and demonstrating techniques.
- Sustain a regular practice of training and physical activity.
- Report for duty in adequate physical condition to work safely on the mat.
- Take care of their health, rest, and nutrition as part of their preparation for work.
- Report to their **Superior** any injury or physical condition that limits the performance of their duties.

<Callout type="info">

The instructor's physical fitness is part of the instruction: it supports technical demonstration, safety on the mat, and the professional standard students take as a reference.

</Callout>

## Annual evaluation

All the competencies described on this page are verified annually through the instructor evaluation, which includes a practical class, a written English exam, and a physical test.

See [Annual Evaluation](./annual-evaluation) for the stages and criteria.

## Instructor responsibility

<Callout type="warn">

Professional development is the individual responsibility of each instructor.

The program provides guidance and support through the chain of command, but the initiative to study, practice, and stay current rests with the instructor.

</Callout>
