Maintaining and developing the competencies required by the role is the responsibility of every instructor.

The Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program expects its instructors to improve continuously on three fronts: **English communication**, **staying technically up to date** — including the program and the teaching methodology — and **physical fitness**. All of these competencies directly affect the quality of instruction, student safety and the image of the program.

<Callout type="info">

Professional development is not a one-time requirement. It is a permanent commitment, monitored throughout the instructor's time in the program.

</Callout>

## English communication

English is the working language at the training bases. It is the language the instructor uses to conduct the class, guide the students, complete the documentation and communicate with management.

### Requirements

Therefore, every instructor must:

- Conduct the class and communicate with students in English.
- Understand instructions, announcements and guidance received in English.
- Produce clear and correct written communication in English in reports and official channels.
- Actively work to improve their English — spoken and written — if their current level is not sufficient for the role.

### Continuous improvement

If their English level does not yet fully meet the requirements of the role, the instructor must maintain an active learning process through:

- Self-study and daily practice.
- Language courses or programs.
- Use of English in everyday communication with colleagues and students.

<Callout type="warn">

Limited English proficiency is not accepted as a permanent condition. The instructor is expected to make a continuous and visible effort to improve.

</Callout>

---

## Staying technically up to date

Jiu-Jitsu evolves constantly, and so do the rules that govern training and competition. Keeping up with this evolution is part of the instructor's role.

### Requirements

Every instructor must:

- Keep up with the official Jiu-Jitsu rules and their updates.
- Stay informed about the technical evolution of the sport.
- Know the official Palms Sports teaching program and keep up with its updates.
- Master and apply the teaching methodology adopted by the program, incorporating the revisions that are published.
- Maintain and improve their own training and technical level over time.
- Keep up with the announcements and updates published by the program.
- Share relevant updates on rules and methodology with the team.

### Areas to keep up with

| Area | What to keep up with |
| ---- | -------------------- |
| Rules | Official regulations, changes to scoring, fouls and refereeing criteria. |
| Technique | Evolution of positions, systems and trends applied to competition and training. |
| Methodology | Teaching methods, teaching progression and adaptation to the students' level. |
| Program | Internal announcements, procedure updates and manual standards. |

<Callout type="info">

Staying up to date is not an optional bonus — it is an essential part of the role. It is what supports the instructor's technical authority in front of the students, raises the standard of the whole team and ensures the credibility of the Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program with the training bases.

</Callout>

## Physical fitness

The instructor conducts classes, demonstrates techniques and trains alongside the students. Maintaining good physical condition is part of the role and supports both the quality of instruction and the example set for the students.

### Requirements

Every instructor must:

- Maintain a level of physical fitness suitable for conducting classes and demonstrating techniques.
- Keep a regular routine of training and physical activity.
- Report for duty in adequate physical condition to work safely on the mat.
- Take care of their health, rest and nutrition as part of preparing for work.
- Report to their **immediate superior** any injury or physical condition that could limit the performance of their duties.

<Callout type="info">

The instructor's physical fitness is part of the instruction: it supports technical demonstration, safety on the mat and the professional standard that students take as a reference.

</Callout>

## Annual evaluation

All the competencies described on this page are checked every year through the instructor evaluation, which includes a practical class, a written English test and a physical test.

## Instructor responsibility

<Callout type="warn">

Professional development is the individual responsibility of each instructor.

The program provides guidance and support through the chain of command, but the initiative to study, practice and stay up to date rests with the instructor themselves.

</Callout>
