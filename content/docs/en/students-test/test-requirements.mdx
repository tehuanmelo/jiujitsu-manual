---
title: Test Requirements
description: Minimum requirements for students to be eligible for promotion tests.
---

Before a student is approved for a promotion test, the instructor must verify that all required classes, competitions, and evaluations have been completed for the student's current belt level.

<Callout type="warn">

A student is **not eligible** for a promotion test until **all minimum requirements** have been completed and verified.

</Callout>

## Requirements by Belt Level

### White Belt

| Promotion | Minimum Requirements |
| ---------- | -------------------- |
| **Stripe 1** | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes |
| **Stripe 2** | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes |
| **Stripe 3** | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes |
| **Stripe 4** | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes |
| **White → Blue Belt** | • 3 Jiu-Jitsu classes<br />• Participation in **2 competitions** |

### Blue Belt

| Promotion | Minimum Requirements |
| ---------- | -------------------- |
| **Stripe 1** | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes |
| **Stripe 2** | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes |
| **Stripe 3** | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes |
| **Stripe 4** | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes |
| **Blue → Purple Belt** | • 3 Jiu-Jitsu classes<br />• Participation in **2 competitions** |

### Purple Belt

| Promotion | Minimum Requirements |
| ---------- | -------------------- |
| **Stripe 1** | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes |
| **Stripe 2** | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes |
| **Stripe 3** | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes |
| **Stripe 4** | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes |
| **Purple → Brown Belt** | • 3 Jiu-Jitsu classes<br />• 20 Close Combat classes<br />• Written Rules Test<br />• Participation in **3 competitions** |

### Brown Belt

| Promotion | Minimum Requirements |
| ---------- | -------------------- |
| **Stripe 1** | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes |
| **Stripe 2** | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes |
| **Stripe 3** | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes |
| **Stripe 4** | 30 Jiu-Jitsu classes |
| **Brown → Black Belt** | • 3 Jiu-Jitsu classes<br />• 30 Close Combat classes<br />• Written Rules Test<br />• Practical Referee Test<br />• Participation in **3 competitions** |
## Instructor Responsibility

<Callout type="info">

Before signing or approving a promotion test, instructors must verify that the student has successfully completed **every required item** for that promotion.

</Callout>

## Important

<Callout type="warn">

Authorizing a promotion test for a student who does not meet the minimum requirements compromises the integrity of the evaluation process.

</Callout>
