Every Jiu-Jitsu class must follow the official lesson structure to ensure consistency, teaching quality, and an effective learning experience across all training bases.

Unless otherwise instructed by management, all instructors must follow the sequence described below.

<Callout type="warn">

All instructors must be on the mat wearing the complete official uniform **at least 10 minutes before the scheduled class start time**.

See the [Dress Code](/docs/professional-conduct/dress-code#during-classes). 

</Callout>

## Class Structure

Every Jiu-Jitsu class must have a duration of **80 minutes** and follow the official lesson structure below. This standardized structure ensures consistency, effective instruction, and a uniform learning experience across all training bases.



<Steps>

<Step>

### Joint Activation

Prepare students physically for training by increasing mobility and activating the major joints used during practice.

**Objectives:**

- Increase joint mobility.
- Improve range of motion.
- Reduce injury risk.
- Prepare the body for physical activity.

</Step>

<Step>

### Warm-up

Increase body temperature and prepare students for the technical portion of the class through dynamic movement.

**Examples include:**

- Light jogging.
- Movement drills.
- Coordination exercises.
- Partner warm-up drills.
- Dynamic stretching.

</Step>

<Step>

### Fundamentals

Review and reinforce the program's fundamental movements.

**Examples include:**

- Breakfalls.
- Rolls.
- Hip escapes.
- Technical stand-up.
- Basic movement drills.

</Step>

<Step>

### Strike and Self-Defense

The content of this section depends on the day of the week.

| Day | Content |
| --- | ------- |
| Monday | Strike Training |
| Tuesday | Self-Defense |
| Wednesday | Strike Training |
| Thursday | Self-Defense |
| Friday | Free Training |
| Saturday | Free Training |
| Sunday | Free Training |

</Step>

<Step>

### Technical Development

This phase is dedicated to teaching the **official technique of the day**.

The instructor must follow the technique assigned for that day in the official Jiu-Jitsu Program manual. Techniques must not be replaced or modified unless specifically instructed by management.

To ensure that all students learn at the same pace, instructors should teach the technique using a step-by-step approach.

The recommended teaching sequence is:

1. Demonstrate the complete technique.
2. Break the technique into small steps.
3. Explain one step at a time.
4. Have all students perform the same step simultaneously.
5. Observe and correct execution.
6. Continue only after the group has understood the previous step.
7. Finish by having students perform the complete technique.

This teaching method keeps the class synchronized and allows the instructor to effectively supervise every student.

</Step>

<Step>

### Technical Drill

The Technical Drill is the sparring phase of the class, where students apply the techniques learned in a live training environment.

Depending on the lesson objective, the instructor may conduct either:

- **Specific Training** — Sparring with a defined objective or starting position.
- **Full Sparring** — A complete Jiu-Jitsu match following the official rules.

During this phase, the instructor must:

- Monitor all students continuously.
- Ensure a safe training environment.
- Correct technical mistakes when appropriate.
- Encourage active participation from every student.
- Ensure that all students remain engaged throughout the training session.

</Step>

<Step>

### Cool Down

Finish the class with an appropriate cool-down.

**Examples include:**

- Light stretching.
- Breathing exercises.
- Brief review of the lesson.
- Questions and answers when appropriate.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="error">

**Classes shorter than 80 minutes are strictly prohibited.**

Every Jiu-Jitsu class **must** last **80 minutes**. Conducting classes with a shorter duration is **completely unacceptable** and is considered a violation of the official program standards. All instructors are required to follow the approved lesson structure in full.

</Callout>


## Instructor Responsibilities

During every class, instructors must:

- Arrive on the mat **at least 10 minutes before** the scheduled class.
- Wear the complete official uniform.
- Follow the official lesson structure.
- Teach the official technique of the day.
- Maintain control of the class at all times.
- Ensure all students actively participate.
- Correct students respectfully.
- Promote a safe and professional learning environment.

---

## Student Participation

Every student must remain actively engaged throughout the class.

Students must **never** remain idle while others are training.

If necessary, instructors should:

- Organize additional training pairs.
- Adjust group sizes.
- Assign individual drills.
- Rotate students appropriately.

<Callout type="info">

Every student should be practicing or receiving instruction at all times during the class.

</Callout>

---

## Teaching Method

To maintain organization and ensure that all students learn at the same pace, instructors should teach techniques in small sequential steps.

A recommended teaching sequence is:

1. Demonstrate the complete technique.
2. Break the technique into small steps.
3. Explain one step at a time.
4. Have all students perform the same step simultaneously.
5. Observe and correct execution.
6. Continue to the next step only after the group has understood the previous one.
7. Finish by practicing the complete technique.

This method keeps the class synchronized and allows the instructor to monitor every student effectively.

---

## Professional Communication

Instructors must communicate clearly, respectfully, and professionally throughout the class.

During instruction:

- Use simple and objective language.
- Speak clearly and confidently.
- Give constructive corrections.
- Encourage students respectfully.
- Maintain a positive learning environment.
- Avoid unnecessary conversations unrelated to the class.
- Avoid inappropriate or offensive language.

For additional communication standards, see the [Instructor Conduct](./instructor-conduct) and [Chain of Command](./chain-of-command) sections.

---

## Important

<Callout type="warn">

The official lesson structure is mandatory.

All instructors are expected to follow the lesson sequence, teach the official curriculum, maintain active student participation, and conduct every class with professionalism and consistency.

</Callout>
