An Incident Report must be completed whenever an unusual event or operational issue occurs during the execution of the Jiu-Jitsu Program. Reporting incidents helps maintain accurate records, supports operational improvements, and ensures that management is informed of situations that may affect classes, instructors, students, or facilities.

<Callout type="warn">

Every operational incident must be reported **as soon as possible**. Do not wait until the end of the day to complete the report.

</Callout>

## When an Incident Report Is Required

An Incident Report must be completed whenever an unusual event occurs that affects the normal operation of the program.

Examples include:

- Problems with the training facility.
- Unexpected inspections.
- Unexpected class cancellations.
- Equipment damage or malfunction.
- Safety concerns within the training area.
- Problems affecting the scheduled class.
- Security-related incidents.
- Any operational event that is unusual or requires management awareness.

<Callout type="info">

If an event is unusual, affects normal operations, or may require future reference, an Incident Report should be completed.

</Callout>

---

## Reporting Procedure

Follow the steps below whenever an operational incident occurs.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Manage the Situation

- Ensure the safety of students and instructors.
- Take any immediate action necessary to minimize the impact of the incident.
- Inform the appropriate military or base personnel if required.
- Continue or suspend the class as appropriate for the situation.

</Step>

<Step>

### Complete the Incident Report

Complete the Incident Report as soon as possible after the event.

The report should include:

- Date and time of the incident.
- Training base.
- Instructor responsible for the class.
- Description of the incident.
- People involved.
- Actions taken.
- Current status of the situation.

</Step>

<Step>

### Submit the Report

After completing the report:

- Submit the original Incident Report to the base command.
- Store one copy in the Jiu-Jitsu room for record keeping.
- Complete and submit the online Incident Report.

</Step>
</Steps>

All three steps are mandatory.

---

## Instructor Responsibility

The instructor conducting the class is responsible for ensuring that:

- The Incident Report is completed accurately.
- The report is submitted without unnecessary delay.
- The base command receives the original report.
- A copy is stored in the Jiu-Jitsu room.
- The online Incident Report is completed and submitted.

Failure to report operational incidents may affect safety, administrative records, and the effective management of the Jiu-Jitsu Program.

---

## Incident Report vs. Injury Report

| Situation | Report Type |
| ---------- | ----------- |
| Student injury or physical accident | [Injury Report](./injury-report) |
| Operational issue or unusual event | **Incident Report** |

<Callout type="info">

If a facility incident results in a student injury, **both an Incident Report and an Injury Report may be required**, depending on the circumstances.

</Callout>

---

## Important

<Callout type="error">

Every unusual operational event that affects the Jiu-Jitsu Program must be documented.

When in doubt, complete an Incident Report and notify your immediate leader.

Never leave an operational incident undocumented.

</Callout>
