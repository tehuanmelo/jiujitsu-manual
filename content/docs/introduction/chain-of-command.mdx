title: Chain of Command
description: Communication hierarchy and reporting procedures for all instructors.
---

The Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program follows a defined chain of command to ensure effective communication, efficient decision-making, and clear accountability across all operational bases.

Every instructor is expected to respect this hierarchy and communicate through the appropriate management level.

<Callout type="warn">

Always contact your **immediate responsible** first. Do not bypass the chain of command unless specifically instructed or authorized to do so.

</Callout>

## Purpose

The chain of command establishes a clear reporting structure that allows information to flow efficiently throughout the organization. Following this structure helps ensure that operational matters are resolved quickly and by the appropriate personnel.

Every management level has specific responsibilities, authority, and information relevant to its role.

---

## Communication Hierarchy

| Level | Position | Primary Responsibility |
| -----: | -------- | ---------------------- |
| I | **Head Coach** | Direct supervision of instructors and daily operational support. |
| II | **Advisor** | Technical guidance and operational assistance. |
| III | **Team Leader** | Team coordination, scheduling, and operational reporting. |
| IV | **Supervisor** | Operational supervision, performance monitoring, and technical oversight. |
| V | **Technical Manager** | Management of the technical department and staff. |
| VI | **CEO / General Manager** | Strategic leadership and overall company management. |

---

## Communication Guidelines

When communicating about operational matters, instructors must:

- Contact their **immediate responsible** first.
- Follow the established chain of command at all times.
- Escalate an issue only when instructed to do so or when the immediate responsible is unavailable.
- Use only the company's official communication channels.
- Keep their immediate supervisor informed about matters affecting students, classes, operations, safety, or administration.

---

## When to Follow the Chain of Command

The chain of command should be followed whenever an instructor needs:

- Technical guidance.
- Operational support.
- Schedule changes.
- Leave or absence approval.
- Equipment requests.
- Administrative assistance.
- Incident or injury reporting.
- Student-related guidance.
- Approval for operational decisions.
- Any matter requiring management involvement.

---

## Why the Chain of Command Matters

Following the chain of command provides several important benefits:

| Benefit | Description |
| -------- | ----------- |
| Efficient Communication | Information reaches the appropriate person without unnecessary delays. |
| Faster Resolution | Issues are handled by the person responsible for that level of authority. |
| Clear Accountability | Every level understands its responsibilities and decision-making authority. |
| Better Organization | Communication follows a consistent and professional process across all bases. |
| Operational Consistency | Procedures are applied uniformly throughout the Jiu-Jitsu Program. |

---

## Typical Communication Flow

In most situations, communication should follow this sequence:

```text
Instructor
        │
        ▼
    Head Coach
            │
            ▼
         Advisor
              │
              ▼
         Team Leader
                  │
                  ▼
              Supervisor
                      │
                      ▼
               Technical Manager
                              │
                              ▼
                     CEO / General Manager
```

Normally, instructors communicate with their **Head Coach**. If an issue requires a higher level of management, it will be escalated through the chain of command.

---

## Prohibited Actions

<Callout type="warn">

The following actions are not permitted unless specifically authorized:

- Contacting higher management without first informing your immediate responsible.
- Bypassing one or more levels of the chain of command.
- Requesting approvals from a higher authority without following the proper reporting process.
- Ignoring instructions from your immediate responsible.

</Callout>

---

## Exceptional Situations

If your immediate responsible is unavailable or has specifically instructed you to contact another member of management, you may communicate directly with the appropriate person.

Whenever possible, keep your immediate responsible informed of any communication made outside the normal reporting structure.

---

## Summary

<Callout type="info">

Following the chain of command promotes professionalism, improves communication, speeds up problem resolution, and ensures that every operational matter is handled by the appropriate level of management.

Respecting this process is a responsibility of every instructor and contributes to the success of the Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program.

</Callout>
