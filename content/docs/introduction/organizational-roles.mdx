---

The Palms Sports Jiu-Jitsu Program operates under a structured chain of command. Each role has specific responsibilities and authority to ensure effective communication, operational efficiency, and consistent leadership throughout the organization.

## Organizational Structure

| Level | Position | Primary Responsibility |
| -----: | -------- | ---------------------- |
| VI | **CEO / General Manager** | Provides the strategic direction of the company and oversees the organization's overall performance. |
| V | **Technical Manager** | Manages the technical department, including hiring, promotions, disciplinary actions, and staff development. |
| IV | **Supervisor** | Supervises daily operations, monitors performance, prepares reports, and provides technical and disciplinary guidance. |
| III | **Team Leader** | Coordinates instructors, manages schedules, communicates organizational information, and reports incidents, accidents, and team performance. |
| II | **Advisor** | Supports instructors and Team Leaders by providing technical guidance, solving operational problems, and assisting with administrative decisions. |
| I | **Head Coach** | Directly supervises instructors, provides technical leadership, mentors coaches, and ensures training quality at the assigned base. |

---

## Role Descriptions

### VI — CEO / General Manager

The CEO / General Manager is responsible for the company's strategic direction. This role establishes organizational objectives and ensures that the company operates efficiently to achieve the best possible results.

---

### V — Technical Manager

The Technical Manager oversees the technical operations of the organization and is responsible for:

- Hiring new staff.
- Evaluating employee performance.
- Promotions and career development.
- Disciplinary actions when necessary.
- Overall management of the technical team.

---

### IV — Supervisor

The Supervisor is responsible for overseeing daily operations and ensuring that company procedures are followed.

Responsibilities include:

- Supervising operational activities.
- Monitoring instructor performance.
- Preparing reports.
- Providing technical guidance.
- Supporting disciplinary procedures when required.

---

### III — Team Leader

The Team Leader coordinates instructors within their assigned group or area.

Responsibilities include:

- Coordinating schedules.
- Communicating organizational information.
- Collecting operational information.
- Reporting student incidents and accidents.
- Reporting instructor performance.
- Supporting daily operational activities.

---

### II — Advisor

The Advisor provides technical and operational support to instructors and Team Leaders.

Responsibilities include:

- Assisting with problem solving.
- Providing technical guidance.
- Supporting operational decisions.
- Advising management on administrative and technical matters.
- Helping ensure that program objectives are achieved.

---

### I — Head Coach

The Head Coach is the instructor's direct supervisor and the first point of contact within the chain of command.

Responsibilities include:

- Supervising instructors at the assigned base.
- Providing technical leadership.
- Mentoring and developing instructors.
- Ensuring training quality and consistency.
- Supporting instructors with operational and technical matters.
- Escalating issues to higher management when necessary.

<Callout type="info">

For operational matters, instructors should always contact their **Head Coach** first and follow the established chain of command.

</Callout>
