---
title: Dress Code
description: Dress code requirements for instructors at all bases facilities.
---


All instructors must follow the official dress code to maintain a professional appearance and represent the Jiu-Jitsu Program at all times.

## Entering the Base

When entering any training base, facility, or dependency, instructors must wear appropriate sports clothing.

- Sports clothing is mandatory.
- Pants and closed shoes are mandatory.
- Adidas sportswear is preferred whenever possible.

<Callout type="error">
Shorts and flip-flops are **not** allowed while walking around the base.
</Callout>

---

## Areas Where Shorts Are Allowed

Shorts may only be worn during sports activities, in areas such:

- Gym
- Sports Field
- Running Tracks



<Callout type="warn">
Outside of these areas, instructors must wear long pants.
</Callout>

---

## During Classes

While teaching, instructors must wear the complete official company uniform:

| Uniform | Requirement |
| ------------ | ----------- |
| Official company Jiu-Jitsu gi (kimono) | **Mandatory** |
| Official company rash guard | **Mandatory** |
| Belt corresponding to instructor rank | **Mandatory** |
| Company name tag with instructor name and PS number | **Mandatory** |

---


## Footwear During Classes

Flip-flops are allowed only inside the Jiu-Jitsu training area and may be worn while moving around the facility before or after training.

Outside the Jiu-Jitsu facility, instructors must wear closed shoes.
