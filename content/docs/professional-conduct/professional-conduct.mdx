title: Behavior
description: Behavior and professional conduct standards for all Jiu-Jitsu instructors.
---

Before teaching or representing the Jiu-Jitsu Program, every instructor is expected to maintain the highest standards of professionalism, discipline, and respect. Instructors represent not only themselves but also the Palms Sports, and the Jiu-Jitsu Program.

The standards described in this document apply at all times while on duty, including during classes, tests, competitions, meetings, and when inside any Training facility.

<Callout type="warn">

Professional conduct is a fundamental responsibility of every instructor. Failure to follow these standards may result in disciplinary action.

</Callout>

## Professional Behavior

Every instructor is expected to:

- Treat all students, colleagues, and military personnel with respect.
- Maintain a professional attitude at all times.
- Communicate in a respectful, calm, and courteous manner.
- Be patient and willing to answer questions.
- Create a positive, safe, and welcoming learning environment.
- Demonstrate discipline, integrity, and professionalism through their actions.
- Lead by example both inside and outside the training area.

---

## Student Relations

The instructor's primary responsibility is to support student development.

Every instructor should:

- Listen carefully to students.
- Be approachable and available to help.
- Encourage learning through positive instruction.
- Correct mistakes respectfully.
- Treat every student fairly and equally.
- Maintain a professional relationship with all students.

<Callout type="info">

Students should always feel respected, supported, and comfortable asking questions during training.

</Callout>

---

## Personal Appearance

Instructors are expected to maintain a clean and professional appearance.

### Grooming Standards

| Requirement | Standard |
| ------------ | -------- |
| Hair | Clean, neat, and well groomed |
| Beard | Clean, trimmed, and well maintained |
| Personal hygiene | Must always be maintained |
| Uniform | Clean and in good condition |

### Tattoos

Visible tattoos are **not permitted**.

Any tattoo must remain completely covered while on duty, including during classes, meetings, competitions, and when inside National Guard facilities.

<Callout type="warn">

All visible tattoos must be covered at all times while on duty.

</Callout>

---

## Dress Code

Every instructor must follow the official dress code while on duty.

Requirements include appropriate sports clothing when entering the base, the official company uniform during classes, and compliance with all appearance standards.

➡️ See the [Dress Code](./dress-code) for complete requirements.

---


## Working with Colleagues

Professional teamwork is essential for the success of the Jiu-Jitsu Program.

Every instructor is expected to:

- Treat colleagues with respect.
- Cooperate with other instructors.
- Share knowledge and assist teammates whenever possible.
- Maintain a positive working environment.
- Resolve disagreements professionally.
- Support new instructors when guidance is needed.
- Work together to achieve the objectives of the program.

---

## Following Instructions

Instructors are expected to follow all lawful instructions issued by their supervisors.

This includes:

- Following operational procedures.
- Completing assigned duties on time.
- Respecting schedules and planning.
- Supporting official program decisions.
- Following Palms Sports policies.

If clarification is required, instructors should respectfully ask their supervisor before taking action.

---

## Professional Ethics

Every instructor must:

- Act honestly and with integrity.
- Protect confidential information.
- Avoid behavior that could damage the reputation of the program.
- Never misuse their position or authority.
- Remain impartial and professional when making decisions.
- Set a positive example for students and colleagues.

---

## Prohibited Conduct

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Disrespectful language or behavior.
- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Aggressive or inappropriate communication.
- Ignoring instructions from supervisors.
- Bypassing the chain of command without authorization.
- Creating conflict among colleagues.
- Wearing inappropriate clothing while on duty.
- Failing to maintain professional appearance standards.
- Leaving visible tattoos uncovered.
- Any conduct that damages the image of the Jiu-Jitsu Program, or Palms Sports.

---

## Instructor Commitment

<Callout type="info">

By serving as an instructor, you commit to:

- Maintaining the highest standards of professionalism.
- Treating everyone with respect.
- Following the chain of command.
- Working collaboratively with your colleagues.
- Following the official dress code.
- Supporting the mission and values of the Jiu-Jitsu Program.
- Representing Palms Sports with integrity at all times.

</Callout>
